# The arrow in the other player's hand, before it goes on the string.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-arrow-hand
#
# A VR archer holds an arrow in the hand from the moment the bow is drawn, and again after each shot.
# The archer's game finds it (its "ArrowNode" at the hand's hold node, with its model shown) and says so in
# ArrowNockedRequest, with where the arrow sits against the hand; the other game attaches the arrow on its copy and keeps
# it there. Before this, nothing was sent for an arrow in the hand, and the other player saw an empty hand.
#
# First the bot is the archer: its copy here must hold the arrow where the bot says, then lay it on the string.
# Then the game's player draws a bow, and the bot must hear of the arrow in that player's hand.
log live arrow hand
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

log -- a hunting bow and iron arrows for this bot
additem 13985 1
additem 1397D 20
wait 3
equip 13985 both
equip 1397D
wait 5
log REQUIRE copy equipped 13985
log REQUIRE copy equipped 1397D

log -- the copy draws in front of the headset, in an archer's pose (as live-arrow-nock)
draw on
wait 1
teleport away
wait 6
teleport back
wait 8
infront 120
wait 3
capture other 12
wait 1
handtargets -8 45 110 6 8 118
holdpose on
replay me 2
wait 3
log DO face copy
wait 3

log -- an arrow in the copy's right hand, its head 67 units from the hand bone
nock hand right
wait 3
log CHECK logged ArrowNocked: \w+ \(server \w+\): arrow held in the hand
wait 2
# Where this game drew it: the head as far from the copy's hand as the bot said, 67 units times the hand bone's scale.
# The copy's hand scale is under 1 (57 units on 440f5e0), so anything from 40 to 79 is the arrow in the hand.
log CHECK logged ArrowNocked: \w+ arrow held in the right hand as its owner holds it, its head [4-7][0-9] units from the hand
log SHOT arrow-in-hand of copy
wait 2

log -- from the hand to the string: the same arrow, laid along the bow
nock on
wait 3
log CHECK logged ArrowNocked: \w+ \(server \w+\): arrow put on the string
wait 2
log CHECK logged ArrowNocked: \w+ arrow laid from the
log SHOT arrow-on-string of copy
nock off
wait 3
log CHECK logged ArrowNocked: \w+ \(server \w+\): arrow taken off the string
holdpose off
wait 1

log -- the game's player draws a bow: the arrow in that player's hand goes to the bot
log DO console player.additem 13985 1
log DO console player.additem 1397D 20
wait 2
log DO console player.equipitem 13985
log DO console player.equipitem 1397D
wait 2
log DO papyrus Actor DrawWeapon 14
wait 6
log CHECK logged ArrowNocked: sent this player's arrow in the hand \(right hand
log CHECK bot logged Arrow of \d+: in the hand \(right hand, its head at .*, ([4-9][0-9]|1[0-4][0-9]) units from the hand bone\)
log CHECK game alive
log CHECK game inworld
log done
stop
