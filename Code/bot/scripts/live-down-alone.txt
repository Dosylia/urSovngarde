# Nobody left: the game's player is down when the only other member, this bot, leaves the party.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-down-alone
#
# The server checks its downed players once a second (ReviveService::CheckDowned): a party with nobody standing respawns,
# even one left with a single member. The player must not wait out the whole minute for a revive that cannot come.
log live down alone
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

log -- the player goes down
log DO console player.damageav health 100000
waitfor downstate other down 20
expect downstate other down
wait 3

log -- this bot leaves the party: nobody is left to revive the player
party leave
wait 4
log CHECK logged PlayerService: nobody is left to revive the player; respawning
wait 12
log CHECK logged PlayerService: respawning player
log CHECK player alive
wait 3
log CHECK game alive
log done
stop
