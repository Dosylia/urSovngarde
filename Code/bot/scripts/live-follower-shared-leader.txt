# A follower in both players' saves: only the party leader's exists, and the other player's comes back when the leader's goes.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-follower-shared-leader
#
# The bot leads the party and has Lydia too. The game's own Lydia is hidden, with her things kept, and a copy shows the leader's.
# When the leader's player dismisses her, the game's player has her back as their follower.
party lead
log live follower shared leader
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 3

log -- the game drops its connection (F6), so the party passes to the bot; then it connects again, as a member
log DO f6
wait 10
log DO f6
wait 15
ownership accept
stay

log DO god on
log -- Lydia next to the player, this game's own follower
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo A2C94 14 f:100 f:0 f:0 b:false
log DO papyrus DialogueFollowerScript SetFollower 750BA A2C94
waitfor known ref:A2C94 40
wait 6

log -- the leader has her too
claim ref:A2C94 follower
waitfor ownedby ref:A2C94 me 20
expect ownedby ref:A2C94 me
wait 5
log CHECK logged Follower: A2C94 \(server id \w+\) is player \w+'s; copy \w+ shows her
log CHECK ref A2C94 disabled
# The driver reads the checks as they are logged, so the bot waits before it changes what they read.
wait 2

log -- the leader's player dismisses theirs: this player's own comes back as their follower
follower ref:A2C94 off
wait 10
log CHECK ref A2C94 enabled
log CHECK logged Follower: told the server that A2C94 \(server id \w+\) is this player's follower
waitfor ownedby ref:A2C94 other 20
expect ownedby ref:A2C94 other
log CHECK game alive
log DO god off
wait 2
log done
stop
