# One half of the nobody-left test: this bot is down when the only other member leaves the party.
#
#   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File Tools\VR\run-bot-tests.ps1 -Pair downedleave
#
# Run alone, not in run-all-pairs: the test server puts every bot in one party.
# The server checks its downed players once a second (ReviveService::CheckDowned): alone and down, nobody is left to revive this bot.
log downedleave actor
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn
record downedleave actor
waitfor players >= 1 30
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 3

log -- going down
down
waitfor downstate me down 10
expect downstate me down

log -- the other side leaves the party: nobody is left, and this bot respawns at once, with no safe point of its own
waitfor downstate me fallen 15
expect downstate me fallen
expect respawnpoint me none
expect connected
record off
report bot-downedleave-actor.txt
log done
stop
