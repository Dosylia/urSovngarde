# A downed player's copy must get up on the other player's game once the player is revived.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-revive-copy
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py --expect-fail live-revive-copy     on a build without the fix: must be RED
#
# A player reported that after a revive the other player still saw them lying down (v1.9.6, 2026-10-24).
# The bot draws no copy, so the game's player goes down while the bot records it, the bot revives it and records on,
# and then plays the recording as its own character: the game's copy of the bot goes down and is revived.
#
# A run counts only when (REQUIRE): the recording holds the owner's get-up, and the copy lay down at some point.
# It is then judged on the copy being on its feet at the end.
log live revive copy
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

log -- the game's player goes down while the bot records it
log DO console player.damageav health 100000
capture other 8
log -- the bot revives it and records on through the get-up
revive other
capture other 10 keep
expect downstate other revived
log CHECK logged PlayerService: revived by player
log REQUIRE bot logged Captured action names of \w+: .*\bbleedOutStop\b

log -- the bot plays the recording as its own character; the game's copy of it goes down and gets up
log DO watch copy 24
replay me
wait 3
log SHOT after of copy
log REQUIRE copy went down
log CHECK copy standing
log CHECK game alive
# The game checks the copy only once its 24 s watch is over: the copy must still be there.
wait 20
log done
stop
