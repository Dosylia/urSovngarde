# Frost and shock spells on the other player do damage, as fire does.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-pvp-spell-elements
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py --expect-fail live-pvp-spell-elements     on a build without the fix: must be RED
#
# A damage spell goes as a health change (PvP on, as on the rig's server): a touch spell once, an aimed one for each of its hits.
# Frost and shock lower health as dual value modifiers (with stamina or magicka).
# Here the player casts a frost touch (3384A AAAFrostTouch, 10), a shock touch (50CFA, a storm atronach's, 20) and Fireball (1C789, 40)
# at this bot's character.
# The rig's idle controllers aim a cast loosely: Ice Spike, Ice Storm and Chain Lightning miss the copy in some runs.
# So frost and shock are touch spells, which land on the target without aim.
# Their effects are dual value modifiers on health, like Ice Spike's and Chain Lightning's.
# Fireball bursts over an area, so it lands without aim too.
# The frost touch goes first, so the first health change that reaches the bot is the frost one.
# A run counts only when (REQUIRE): the fire spell was sent, so the casts reach the copy at all.
log live pvp spell elements
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

# The rig's controllers point straight ahead, and a VR player cannot be turned: the bot's character goes there.
infront 200
wait 3

log -- a frost touch on this bot's character
log DO papyrus Spell Cast 3384A 14 copy
wait 6
log CHECK logged PvP: spell 3384A hit remote player
log CHECK bot logged Health change of our [0-9A-F]+ by player \d+: -\d+\.\d
wait 2

log -- a shock touch
log DO papyrus Spell Cast 50CFA 14 copy
wait 6
log CHECK logged PvP: spell 50CFA hit remote player
log CHECK bot logged Health change of our [0-9A-F]+ by player \d+: -20\.0
wait 2

log -- Fireball
log DO papyrus Spell Cast 1C789 14 copy
wait 6
log REQUIRE logged PvP: hit remote player \w+ for 24
log CHECK bot logged Health change of our [0-9A-F]+ by player \d+: -24\.0
wait 3
log CHECK game alive
log done
stop
