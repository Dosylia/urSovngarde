# How a death in a fight looks on the other player's screen: the original thrown by a blast, its copy only told it died.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-death-replay-blast
#
# As live-death-replay, but the original dies by the player's Fire Storm (7A82B), which throws its ragdoll, as a fight's
# blows and spells do. The copy gets the recording and the death, but no blast: it falls where it stands.
# The checks say how far apart the two bodies come to rest and how differently they lie: what the other player sees
# differ from the owner's screen in a fight (sync audit, 2026-10-26: corpses shifted 68 to 220 units after landing).
log live death replay blast
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5
log DO god on

log -- the original: a mage this game runs, 150 units ahead, low on health
log DO place 753CB
wait 8
log DO papyrus Actor SetRestrained placed b:true
log DO papyrus Actor ForceActorValue placed s:Health f:10
wait 3

log -- a Fire Storm kills it while this game samples it and the bot records what this game sends of it
log DO record original placed 12
log DO sleep 2
log DO papyrus Spell Cast 7A82B 14 14
capture newest 12
log REQUIRE bot logged info\] Captured the death of
log SHOT original of placed

log -- the copy: a mage of the bot's own plays the recording, and this game shows it as a copy
npc captured
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 8
log REQUIRE npc standing
wait 4
# The client poses no body out of view, and the bot's mage stands 200 units east of the player, often behind the headset.
log DO look at npc 500 hold 22000
wait 1
log REQUIRE view npc
log DO record copy npc 14
replay npc
wait 6
log SHOT copy of npc
log CHECK npc dead
log CHECK fall copy like original 700
log CHECK pose copy like original 30
log CHECK place copy like original 100
log CHECK still copy 30
log CHECK smooth all
wait 4
log CHECK game alive
log DO god off
log done
stop
