# A copy of an essential NPC that goes down and gets up again must get up here too.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-copy-bleedout
#
# Players saw downed NPCs keep lying on the floor and slide along behind their owner once the owner's NPC had stood up.
# The copy refused the owner's "bleedOutStop" (ReplayDiag), stayed in its bleedout pose, and followed the owner's walk lying down.
# Here the game places a Mistwatch bandit (base C0115, made essential), knocks it down and stands it up while the bot records it.
# Then the bot brings a bandit of its own and replays the recording on it, so the game's copy of it gets the owner's real actions.
# (The server never hands a temporary live actor to another player, so the placed bandit itself cannot be taken over.)
log live copy bleedout
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

log DO god on
log -- a bandit ahead of the player, essential so it goes down instead of dying (the bot's bandit below shares the base)
log DO papyrus ActorBase SetEssential C0115 b:true
log DO place C0115 essential
wait 8

log -- the game knocks it down and stands it up again while the bot records it
log DO sleep 3
log DO papyrus Actor DamageActorValue placed s:Health f:100000
log DO sleep 9
log DO papyrus Actor RestoreActorValue placed s:Health f:100000
capture newest 22
log DO papyrus Actor IsBleedingOut placed

log -- the bot brings its own bandit and replays the recording on it; the game shows a copy of it
npc temp C0115
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 6
# No health left, as a hero beaten down in a fight: the copy goes down by itself, and its own health is what the game checks before it lets it stand up.
npclife alive 0
wait 3
replay npc
wait 3
log DO papyrus Actor IsBleedingOut npc
log SHOT after of npc
log CHECK absent ReplayDiag: 'bleedOutStop' refused
log CHECK game alive
log DO god off
wait 3
log done
stop
