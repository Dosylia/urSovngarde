# A copy of an essential NPC that goes down and gets up again must get up here too.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-copy-bleedout
#
# Players saw downed NPCs keep lying on the floor and slide along behind their owner once the owner's NPC had stood up.
# The copy refused the owner's "bleedOutStop" (ReplayDiag), stayed in its bleedout pose, and followed the owner's walk lying down.
# Here the game places a Lydia (base A2C8E, made essential), knocks her down and stands her up while the bot records it.
# Then the bot brings a Lydia of its own and replays the recording on her, so the game's copy of her gets the owner's real actions.
# A unique base, not a levelled bandit: a levelled copy may pick another race here and is then not fed the owner's actions.
# (The server never hands a temporary live actor to another player, so the placed Lydia itself cannot be taken over.)
log live copy bleedout
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

log DO god on
log -- a Lydia ahead of the player, essential so she goes down instead of dying (the bot's below shares the base)
log DO papyrus ActorBase SetEssential A2C8E b:true
log DO place A2C8E essential
wait 8

log -- the game knocks her down and stands her up again while the bot records it
log DO sleep 3
log DO papyrus Actor DamageActorValue placed s:Health f:100000
log DO sleep 9
log DO papyrus Actor RestoreActorValue placed s:Health f:100000
capture newest 22
log DO papyrus Actor IsBleedingOut placed

log -- the bot brings its own Lydia and replays the recording on her; the game shows a copy of her
npc temp A2C8E
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 6
# No health left, as a hero beaten down in a fight: the copy goes down by itself, and its own health is what the game checks before it lets it stand up.
npclife alive 0
wait 3
# Watched through the whole replay: down in bleedout, then on its feet at the end.
log CHECK npc standing 26
replay npc
wait 3
log DO papyrus Actor IsBleedingOut npc
log SHOT after of npc
# The copy falls into its own bleedout in a ragdoll, so it may refuse the owner's "bleedOutStop"; it must still get up.
log CHECK logged KnockDown: NPC copy \w+ is up again
log CHECK absent KnockDown: NPC copy \w+ still lies
log CHECK absent Base form differs
log CHECK game alive
log DO papyrus ActorBase SetEssential A2C8E b:false
log DO god off
wait 3
log done
stop
