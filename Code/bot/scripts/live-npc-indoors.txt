# An NPC that goes indoors with its owner leaves the screen of a player who stays outside.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-npc-indoors
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py --expect-fail live-npc-indoors     on a build without the change: must be RED
#
# The server passed an NPC's moves only to the players in range of its new cell, and never took it away from those it
# left. A follower that went into a house with its owner stood frozen outside for the other player.
# Now an NPC that goes through a door, out of a player's range, is removed for that player, and a move that brings it
# back sends it to them again.
# Here: the bot brings a bear next to the player outdoors, goes into Helgen's keep with it, and comes back out.
log live npc indoors
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn ownership
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

log DO god on
log -- the bot's bear next to the player
npc temp 23A8A
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 8
log CHECK npc exists
wait 3

log -- the bot goes into Helgen's keep, and its bear with it
stay
cell 5DE24
wait 2
npcmove 0 0 0
wait 6
log CHECK npc gone
wait 3

log -- they come back out
cell out
wait 2
npcmove 0 0 0
wait 10
log CHECK npc exists

wait 3
log CHECK game alive
log DO god off
wait 2
log done
stop
