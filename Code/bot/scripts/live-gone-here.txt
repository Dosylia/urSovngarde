# A reference left standing here when the server drops its character is announced again, so the other player still sees it.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-gone-here
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py --expect-fail live-gone-here     on a build without the fix: must be RED
#
# In play a follower's own game dropped her at a city gate, nobody was in range of her last place, and the server removed her.
# The other game went on running her with no server id, so only that player could see her.
# Here the bot owns Lydia and lets go of her with a last place far from both players, while she stands beside the game's player.
log live gone here
waitfor connected 45
collect ownership party
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
ownership accept
wait 5

log DO god on
log REQUIRE player in Tamriel

log -- Lydia beside the player, no longer its teammate
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo A2C94 14 f:100 f:0 f:0 b:false
log DO papyrus Actor SetPlayerTeammate A2C94 b:false b:false
waitfor known ref:A2C94 40
wait 5

log -- the bot takes her
claim ref:A2C94 follower
stay
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 6

log -- the bot lets go of her, last seen far from both players
release ref:A2C94 at -150000 150000 0
wait 8

log REQUIRE ref A2C94 near player 1500
log -- she is known to the server again, so the bot can see her
waitfor known ref:A2C94 20
expect known ref:A2C94
log CHECK game alive
wait 2
log done
stop
