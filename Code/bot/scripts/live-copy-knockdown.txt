# A copy of an essential NPC that is knocked flat while it is down, then gets up and walks, must get up here too, and
# never slide along the floor.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-copy-knockdown
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py --expect-fail live-copy-knockdown     on a build without the fix: must be RED
#
# Players saw downed NPCs lie flat and slide along behind their owner once the owner's NPC had stood up and walked on.
# live-copy-bleedout covers a plain bleedout; here the owner's NPC is also thrown to the floor while it is down.
# The game places a Lydia (base A2C8E, made essential), knocks her into bleedout, pushes her over, stands her up
# and sets her on the player, while the bot records it.
# A unique base, not a levelled bandit: a levelled copy may pick another race here and is then not fed the owner's actions.
# Then the bot brings a Lydia of its own and replays the recording on it, so the game's copy gets the owner's real actions.
#
# A run counts only when (REQUIRE): the recording holds the owner's fall, the owner's NPC really moved while down, the
# copy is the owner's kind, and the copy lay flat at some point. It is then judged on two things: the lying copy was not
# carried along the floor, and it is on its feet at the end.
log live copy knockdown
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

log DO god on
log -- a Lydia ahead of the player, essential so she goes down instead of dying (the bot's below shares the base)
log DO papyrus ActorBase SetEssential A2C8E b:true
log DO place A2C8E essential
wait 8

log -- the game knocks her down, throws her over, stands her up and sets her on the player while the bot records it
log DO sleep 3
log DO papyrus Actor DamageActorValue placed s:Health f:100000
log DO sleep 4
log DO papyrus ObjectReference PushActorAway 14 placed f:8
log DO sleep 6
log DO papyrus Actor RestoreActorValue placed s:Health f:100000
log DO combat on
log DO sleep 2
log DO papyrus Actor StartCombat placed 14
capture newest 28
log DO combat off
log DO papyrus Actor IsBleedingOut placed
log REQUIRE bot logged Captured action names of \w+: .*\bRagdoll\b
log REQUIRE logged DownedTravel: own NPC \w+ moved \d+ units in \d+ ms while down

log -- the bot brings its own Lydia and replays the recording on her; the game shows a copy of her
npc temp A2C8E
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 6
# No health left, as a hero beaten down in a fight: the copy goes down by itself, as in live-copy-bleedout.
npclife alive 0
wait 3
# The copy's pose through the whole replay and a few seconds after it, judged below.
log DO watch npc 34
replay npc
wait 3
log DO papyrus Actor IsBleedingOut npc
log SHOT after of npc
log REQUIRE absent Base form differs
log REQUIRE npc went down
log CHECK npc slide below 100
log CHECK npc standing
log CHECK game alive
log DO papyrus ActorBase SetEssential A2C8E b:false
log DO god off
wait 3
log done
stop
