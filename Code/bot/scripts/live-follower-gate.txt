# Another player's follower taken through a city gate does not stay standing outside for the player left there.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-follower-gate
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py --expect-fail live-follower-gate     on a build without the fix: must be RED
#
# A walled city is a worldspace of its own, so the place the owner went is not loaded here and the teleport is refused.
# The server then removes her for this player, and she must go with her owner, not stay here as a live, unsynced NPC.
# Here Lydia stands beside the player outdoors, the bot takes her as its follower and goes into Whiterun with her.
log live follower gate
waitfor connected 45
collect ownership party
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
ownership accept
wait 5

log -- Lydia beside the player, no longer its teammate
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo A2C94 14 f:100 f:0 f:0 b:false
log DO papyrus Actor SetActorValue A2C94 s:WaitingForPlayer f:1
log DO papyrus Actor SetPlayerTeammate A2C94 b:false b:false
waitfor known ref:A2C94 40
wait 3
log -- the bot takes her
claim ref:A2C94 follower
stay
wait 6
log REQUIRE ref A2C94 cell same

log -- the bot goes into Whiterun's worldspace with her
cell world 1A26F
npcmove 0 0 0
wait 6
log REQUIRE logged Teleport of actor A2C94 not made: it goes out beyond the loaded area here
log CHECK ref A2C94 cell other
log CHECK game alive

log -- a save made with her there loads: she is moved here after it, so only the load can put her back
log DO console save LiveFollowerGate
wait 6
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo A2C94 14 f:100 f:0 f:0 b:false
wait 3
log REQUIRE ref A2C94 cell same
log DO load save LiveFollowerGate
wait 45
log CHECK game inworld
log CHECK ref A2C94 world 1A26F
wait 2
log done
stop
