# The other half of downedgone: the party's leader, told the other side is down, must then be told it left.
#
# "party lead": this bot keeps the party the server makes for it, so the other side joins it.
party lead
log downedgone watcher
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn
record downedgone watcher
waitfor known other 40
log -- the other side goes down, then leaves the party
waitfor downstate other down 30
expect downstate other down
waitfor downstate other left 20
expect downstate other left
expect connected
record off
report bot-downedgone-watcher.txt
log done
stop
