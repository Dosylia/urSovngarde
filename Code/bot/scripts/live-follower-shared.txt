# A follower in both players' saves stays with one game; the other game's claims are refused.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-follower-shared
#
# Each game asking for her as she went passed her to and fro as fast as messages go.
# Here Lydia (A2C94, the follower in the test save) stands next to the player, and the bot plays the other save.
# It asks for her every second. The game's player had her first and the bot does not lead the party, so she stays.
log live follower shared
waitfor connected 45
collect ownership party
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
ownership accept
stay
wait 5

log DO god on
log -- Lydia next to the player, as the player's follower
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo A2C94 14 f:100 f:0 f:0 b:false
log DO papyrus DialogueFollowerScript SetFollower 750BA A2C94
waitfor known ref:A2C94 40
wait 6
expect ownedby ref:A2C94 other

log -- the other save's game asks for her, over and over
claim ref:A2C94 follower
wait 1
claim ref:A2C94 follower
wait 1
claim ref:A2C94 follower
wait 1
claim ref:A2C94 follower
wait 1
claim ref:A2C94 follower
wait 8

log -- she stays with the game
expect ownedby ref:A2C94 other
log CHECK absent Follower A2C94 \(server id \w+\) was given to player
log CHECK ref A2C94 enabled

wait 2
log CHECK game alive
log DO god off
wait 2
log done
stop
