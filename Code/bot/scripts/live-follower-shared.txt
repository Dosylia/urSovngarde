# A follower in both players' saves ends up with one game; the two games do not pass her to and fro.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-follower-shared
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py --expect-fail live-follower-shared     on a build that always asks back: must be RED
#
# A game asks for its follower back the moment she is given to someone else, which is right for a hand-over at a loading screen.
# With one follower in two saves both games do that, each claim is granted at once, and she changes hands as fast as messages go.
# Here Lydia (A2C94, the follower in the test save) stands next to the player, and the bot plays the other save.
# It asks for her every second. After three passes, each within 2 s of this game getting her, this game must leave her with the bot.
log live follower shared
waitfor connected 45
collect ownership party
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
ownership accept
stay
wait 5

log DO god on
log -- Lydia next to the player
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo A2C94 14 f:100 f:0 f:0 b:false
waitfor known ref:A2C94 40
wait 6
expect ownedby ref:A2C94 other

log -- the other save's game asks for her, over and over
claim ref:A2C94 follower
wait 1
claim ref:A2C94 follower
wait 1
claim ref:A2C94 follower
wait 1
claim ref:A2C94 follower
wait 1
claim ref:A2C94 follower
# Longer than a game's own retry for a follower near its player (5 s).
wait 8

log -- she stays with the bot
expect ownedby ref:A2C94 me
log CHECK logged Follower A2C94 \(server id \w+\) was given to player \w+; asked for back
log CHECK logged Follower A2C94 \(server id \w+\) was given to player \w+; she is that player's follower too

wait 2
log CHECK game alive
log DO god off
wait 2
log done
stop
