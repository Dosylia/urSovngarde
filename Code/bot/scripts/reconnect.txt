# A player whose client dropped and reconnected can come back as a copy the other side can hit but not see, in either direction.
# Nothing in the spawn lines differs from a visible spawn, so the host watches the bot on screen after each reconnect.
# The "CopyDiag:" lines in tp_client.log (one per remote player copy every 5 s) compare the visible copy against the invisible one.
#
# Run:  STBot.exe scripts\reconnect.txt --server 127.0.0.1:10578 --password <sPassword>
# The bot spawns 200 units from the host, cloned from the host's look and outfit.

log standing next to the host for 10 s; look at me, I should be visible
wait 10

log dropping the connection and coming back 2 s later; am I still visible?
disconnect
wait 2
reconnect
wait 15

log second drop, 5 s away this time
disconnect
wait 5
reconnect
wait 15

log walking a small square so the copy can be told apart from a stale one
walk rel 200 0 100
walk rel -200 0 100
wait 10
stop
