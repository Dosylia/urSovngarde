# A downed player whose standing party members are all too far to come (another place, or over 100 m) respawns at once:
# when the last one in reach goes away during the wait, and when it goes down with nobody in reach.
#
#   powershell -File Tools\VR\run-bot-tests.ps1 -Pair downedfar
#
# The leader on the other side reacts to this side's states, so the two need no shared clock.
log downedfar actor
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn
record downedfar actor
waitfor players >= 1 30
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 3
log -- the leader stands by: going down waits
down
waitfor downstate me down 5
expect downstate me down
log -- the leader goes fifteen cells away during the wait: the wait ends
waitfor downstate me died 15
expect downstate me died
log -- the leader is still away: going down again ends at once, never announced as a down
wait 3
down
wait 3
expect downcount me died == 2
expect downcount me down == 1
expect connected
record off
report bot-downedfar-actor.txt
log done
stop
