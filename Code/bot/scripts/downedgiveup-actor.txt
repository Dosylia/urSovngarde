# A downed player whose own game stops waiting (no word from the server, or a bleedout that ended by itself) and respawns.
# The server must end the down then, not when the respawn is over: a revive in between would be accepted and then ignored.
# And the player respawns where the server would have sent it: the party leader's safe point.
#
#   powershell -File Tools\VR\run-bot-tests.ps1 -Pair downedgiveup
log downedgiveup actor
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn
record downedgiveup actor
waitfor players >= 1 30
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 3
log -- going down
down
waitfor downstate me down 10
expect downstate me down
wait 2
log -- this game gives up the wait and respawns
giveup
waitfor downstate me respawned 10
expect downstate me respawned
expect respawnpoint me 500 600
expect connected
record off
report bot-downedgiveup-actor.txt
log done
stop
