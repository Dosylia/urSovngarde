# The caster's perks reach the player it heals, and the healed player's game adds none of its own copy's.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-heal-perks
#
# The healed player's game applies a heal with its copy of the caster, which has no perks, and with the spell's own magnitude
# whatever the message says; so the caster's game sends how much its perks multiplied it, and that is applied in
# AdjustForPerks (MagicTarget.cpp).
# The rig's character has Adamant's Recovery 20 (healing spells 1.5 times as strong); the script adds Adamant's Healer 50
# (C44C8, Restoration spells 1.3 times as strong), and the two multiply.
# Heal Other (12FD2, fire and forget) restores 40 with effect 1CEA7; Healing Hands (4D3F2, concentration) 20 with 1CEA5.
log live heal perks
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5
log REQUIRE copy 3d
wait 1

log -- Heal Other with the character's own perks
log DO papyrus Spell Cast 12FD2 14 copy
wait 3
log CHECK logged Magic: spell 12FD2 on remote player [0-9A-F]+: the caster's perks make it 1\.50 times as strong
expect addtarget 12FD2 1CEA7 1 1.5
wait 1

log -- Healing Hands with the character's own perks
log DO papyrus Spell Cast 4D3F2 14 copy
wait 3
log CHECK logged Magic: spell 4D3F2 on remote player [0-9A-F]+: the caster's perks make it 1\.50 times as strong
expect addtarget 4D3F2 1CEA5 1 1.5
wait 1

log -- Heal Other with Healer 50 added
log DO console player.addperk C44C8
wait 1
log DO papyrus Spell Cast 12FD2 14 copy
wait 3
log CHECK logged Magic: spell 12FD2 on remote player [0-9A-F]+: the caster's perks make it 1\.95 times as strong
expect addtarget 12FD2 1CEA7 2 1.95
log DO console player.removeperk C44C8
wait 2

log -- the bot heals the game's player with Heal Other: 40 without perks, 60 with its caster's 1.5 ("dropped -40" is a rise)
log DO console player.damageav health 150
wait 2
log CHECK player mark
wait 1
effect other 12FD2 1CEA7 40
wait 4
log CHECK player dropped -40
wait 1
log CHECK player mark
wait 1
effect other 12FD2 1CEA7 40 1.5
wait 4
log CHECK player dropped -60
wait 1
log CHECK game alive
log done
stop
