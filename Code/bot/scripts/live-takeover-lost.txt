# What a game does with an NPC it loses while that NPC walks: does its copy hold a stride?
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-takeover-lost
#
# On c382599 it did not: she stood, her feet moving 9 units in 5 s, where an NPC gained mid-walk moved its by 46.
# The game's own Lydia (A2C94, a reference of Skyrim.esm) follows the walking player by her AI, and the bot claims her mid-walk.
# Her teammate flag goes just before the claim, so she goes as a plain NPC and is not asked back.
# The bot then has her stand, sending back what the game itself reported while she stood: real variables, at the real pace, and no VR pose.
# A replay starts from the bot's own place, so the bot waits on the road where she will be at the claim: a real new owner has her where she is.
# `replay` holds the bot's script until it ends, so what happens during it is queued for the driver first.
# It is judged on her legs, read from the game. Run Tools\VR\takeover-probe.py beside it for her speed and position over time.
log live takeover lost
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
ownership accept
stay
wait 5
log DO god on

log -- the game's Lydia, beside the player
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo A2C94 14 f:120 f:0 f:-10 b:false
waitfor known ref:A2C94 40
wait 6
log REQUIRE ref A2C94 near player 400

log -- the bot waits ahead on the road; naming her there only sets where a replay starts, and she stays the game's
teleport 140070 -32550
wait 1
npc A2C94
wait 2
log REQUIRE bot logged NPC registered as actor \w+ \(owner no\)

log -- the bot records her standing, as the game reports it
capture ref:A2C94 6
wait 1
log REQUIRE bot logged Captured \d+ updates of

log -- the player walks up the road, and her AI follows
log DO mark went ref A2C94
log DO key w 10000
wait 3.5
log REQUIRE moved went atleast 300
log REQUIRE ref A2C94 feet moving 1
log DO papyrus Actor SetPlayerTeammate A2C94 b:false b:false
wait 1.5

log -- the bot takes her mid-walk, and has her stand
claim ref:A2C94 follower
wait 1
log DO sleep 4
log CHECK ref A2C94 feet still 5 15
replay npc 4 noactions
log REQUIRE bot logged Actor \w+ is now player \d+'s at epoch \d+ -- that is us
wait 3
log CHECK game alive
log DO god off
wait 2
log done
stop
