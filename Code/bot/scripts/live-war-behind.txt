# A member's game follows the party leader's civil war forward only: a leader whose war is behind undoes nothing here.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-war-behind
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py --expect-fail live-war-behind     on a build without the change: must be RED
#
# The leader is whoever leads the party now, which after a crash or reconnect can be the player whose war is behind.
# That leader's war must not give back a hold this game's war won, nor un-complete or hide an objective finished here.
# Here: the bot leads (this game drops its connection and comes back, and the bot keeps the party), this game is a member.
# This game's war is set in its memory only: hold 16770 owned by side 2, objective 101 of the journal's war quest (29940) shown and completed, objective 102 hidden.
# The bot then sends a war that is behind (every hold to side 1, no objective done), then one that has caught up and gone further.
# The save must have the war quest running and no other war objective completed or failed, or the leader's second war still reads as behind.
# A run counts only when (REQUIRE) the bot did lead the party and the war quest took this setup.
party lead
log live war behind
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 3

log -- the game drops its connection (F6), so the party passes to the bot; then it connects again, as a member
log DO f6
wait 10
log DO f6
wait 15
waitfor known other 60
wait 5
# "info] " is in the bot's own log line, not in its echo of this check.
log REQUIRE bot logged info\] Leading the party

log -- this game's war won a hold and finished its objective; the next objective is not shown yet
log REQUIRE papyrus Quest IsRunning 29940 is true
log DO papyrus Location SetKeywordData 16770 2A456 f:2
log DO papyrus Quest SetObjectiveDisplayed 29940 n:101 b:true b:true
log DO papyrus Quest SetObjectiveCompleted 29940 n:101 b:true
log DO papyrus Quest SetObjectiveDisplayed 29940 n:102 b:false b:true
wait 2
log REQUIRE papyrus Quest IsObjectiveCompleted 29940 n:101 is true
log REQUIRE papyrus Quest IsObjectiveDisplayed 29940 n:102 is false
log REQUIRE papyrus Location GetKeywordData 16770 2A456 is 2
# Checks run while the script goes on, a few seconds behind it: without this wait the leader's war can land before the game is asked.
wait 6

log -- the leader's war is behind: every hold to the other side, no objective done; nothing here may go back
war 1 0
wait 4
log CHECK papyrus Quest IsObjectiveCompleted 29940 n:101 is true
log CHECK papyrus Quest IsObjectiveDisplayed 29940 n:101 is true
log CHECK papyrus Location GetKeywordData 16770 2A456 is 2
log CHECK logged WarState: the leader's war is behind this game's: objective 101 is completed or failed in this game but not in the leader's
wait 6

log -- the leader catches up and goes further: objective 101 done there too, 102 shown; now its holds are taken
war 1 0 101=3 102=1
wait 4
log CHECK papyrus Location GetKeywordData 16770 2A456 is 1
log CHECK papyrus Quest IsObjectiveDisplayed 29940 n:102 is true

wait 2
log CHECK game alive
