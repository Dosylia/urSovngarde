# Party members' scripts often reach the same quest stage on their own: the server relays each stage once.
# This side starts a quest and reaches stage 10 first; the other side then reaches stage 10 too, then stage 20.
# Only stage 20 may come back here: a duplicate stage relayed mid-scene pushed another game's scene past its own scripts.
#
#   powershell -File Tools\VR\run-bot-tests.ps1 -Pair questdup
log questdup actor
waitfor connected 45
record questdup actor
waitfor known other 40
wait 3
log -- this side starts the quest and reaches stage 10 first
quest 1F0 start 0
wait 1
quest 1F0 stage 10
log -- the other side reaches stage 10 on its own, then stage 20: only stage 20 is relayed here
waitfor questupdates 1F0 >= 1 20
wait 3
expect questupdates 1F0 == 1
expect connected
record off
report bot-questdup-actor.txt
log done
stop
