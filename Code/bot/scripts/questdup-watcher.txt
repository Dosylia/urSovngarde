# The other half of questdup: it is told of the other side's start and stage 10, then reaches stage 10 itself (a duplicate,
# not relayed) and stage 20 (relayed).
#
# "party lead": this bot keeps the party the server makes for it, so the other side joins it.
party lead
log questdup watcher
waitfor connected 45
record questdup watcher
waitfor known other 40
log -- the other side's start and stage 10 arrive
waitfor questupdates 1F0 >= 2 30
expect questupdates 1F0 == 2
log -- this side reaches stage 10 too, then stage 20
quest 1F0 stage 10
wait 1
quest 1F0 stage 20
wait 5
expect questupdates 1F0 == 2
expect connected
record off
report bot-questdup-watcher.txt
log done
stop
