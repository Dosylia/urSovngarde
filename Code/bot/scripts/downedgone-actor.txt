# A downed player who leaves the party: the members it leaves behind must be told it is gone, or they go on trying to revive it.
#
#   powershell -File Tools\VR\run-bot-tests.ps1 -Pair downedgone
#
# This side goes down, then leaves the party: alone, nobody can revive it, and it respawns at once (fallen).
log downedgone actor
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn
record downedgone actor
waitfor players >= 1 30
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 3
log -- going down
down
waitfor downstate me down 10
expect downstate me down
wait 2
log -- leaving the party while down
party leave
waitfor downstate me fallen 15
expect downstate me fallen
expect connected
record off
report bot-downedgone-actor.txt
log done
stop
