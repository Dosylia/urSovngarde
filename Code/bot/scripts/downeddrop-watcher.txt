# The other half of downeddrop: the party's leader, told the other side is down, must then be told it left.
#
# "party lead": this bot keeps the party the server makes for it, so the other side joins it.
party lead
log downeddrop watcher
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn
record downeddrop watcher
waitfor known other 40
log -- the other side goes down, then its connection goes
waitfor downstate other down 30
expect downstate other down
waitfor downstate other left 20
expect downstate other left
expect connected
record off
report bot-downeddrop-watcher.txt
log done
stop
