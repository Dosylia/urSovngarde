# Spell damage on an NPC lands once, in the game that runs it: another player's AddTarget is not applied there, its health change is.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-spell-damage
#
# A player's destruction spell on an NPC another game runs reaches that game twice: as an AddTarget at the spell's base
# magnitude, and as health changes of what the caster's game dealt, perks, burn and difficulty included (HookDamageActor).
# The owner must apply only the changes, or the NPC takes the damage twice; HookDamageActor drops damage from another
# player's copy. Here the game owns a Lydia (base A2C8E) at 200 health, and the bot sends both kinds as a caster's game would:
# Firebolt (1C789) with its fire damage effect (1CEA1, 40), then a hit of -30.
log live spell damage
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 3
log -- a Lydia this game owns, at 200 health
log DO place A2C8E
wait 6
log DO papyrus Actor ForceActorValue placed s:Health f:200
wait 4
waitfor health base:A2C8E == 200 10

log -- the bot's Firebolt as an AddTarget: not applied by the NPC's own game
effect base:A2C8E 1C789 1CEA1 40
wait 4
expect health base:A2C8E == 200

log -- the bot's health change of -30: applied
hit base:A2C8E -30
wait 4
expect health base:A2C8E == 170
log CHECK game alive
log done
stop
