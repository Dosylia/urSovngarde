# Right after the party leader arrives, the server waits 10 s before handing the leader the NPCs another game runs.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-npc-grace
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py --expect-fail live-npc-grace     on a build without the change: must be RED
#
# A leader's game claims the NPCs around it as it loads them in. Handed over at once while that game was still
# bringing actors in after a load, sixteen NPCs went back and forth between the games for a minute (MGO).
# Here: Lydia (A2C94, the follower in the test save) waits in Helgen's keep, no longer the player's teammate.
# The player leaves for another interior, so the bot, which stays in the keep, is given her.
# The player comes back: the bot must keep her for about 10 s.
log live npc grace
waitfor connected 45
collect ownership party
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
ownership accept
wait 5

log DO god on
log -- the player into Helgen's keep, Lydia next to the player, waiting there, and no longer the player's teammate
log DO console player.moveto 9B0A5
wait 12
cell 5DE24
stay
wait 3
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo A2C94 14 f:100 f:0 f:0 b:false
log DO papyrus Actor SetActorValue A2C94 s:WaitingForPlayer f:1
log DO papyrus Actor SetPlayerTeammate A2C94 b:false b:false
waitfor known ref:A2C94 40
wait 3

log -- the player leaves for Riverwood's inn; the bot, still in the keep, is given her
# Indoors to indoors: console travel out to Tamriel has hit Community Shaders' crash at a load (+0x1208c8) in the rig.
log DO console coc RiverwoodSleepingGiantInn
waitfor ownedby ref:A2C94 me 40
expect ownedby ref:A2C94 me
wait 5

log -- the player comes back: the bot keeps her for a while, then she goes to the leader
log DO console player.moveto A2C94
waitfor samecell other 60
wait 5
expect ownedby ref:A2C94 me
waitfor ownedby ref:A2C94 other 20
expect ownedby ref:A2C94 other
wait 3
log CHECK ref A2C94 near player 1500

log CHECK game alive
log DO papyrus Actor SetActorValue A2C94 s:WaitingForPlayer f:0
log DO papyrus Actor SetPlayerTeammate A2C94 b:true b:false
log DO god off
# The player is left in the keep: run this script after a fresh `headless.ps1 up`, which loads the save again.
cell out
wait 2
log done
stop
