# A lever pulled in another game moves its bridge here, once and all the way; a lever pulled here reaches the other player.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-lever-bridge
#
# Mistwatch's retractable bridge 917F7 and its lever 91804 stand one cell west of the rig's start (33,-9, BAEA).
# A lever's use is replayed on the user's copy here, and the lever's own script moves the bridge in each game.
# The bridge's open state (1 out, 3 retracted) is sampled about twenty times a second ("DO record ... ref"): each pull
# must show it moving (2 or 4) and end at the other end, with no turn back on the way.
# First the bot pulls the lever (the other player in another game); then this game's own player pulls it.
log live lever bridge
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

log DO god on
log REQUIRE player cell BAE9
log DO papyrus ObjectReference GetOpenState 917F7
wait 3

log -- pulled in another game: the bridge moves here
log DO record pulled-there ref 917F7 10
activate 91804 by me 0 cell BAEA
wait 11
log CHECK door pulled-there once toggled

log -- pulled here: the bridge moves back, and the other player is told of the pull
log DO record pulled-here ref 917F7 10
log DO papyrus ObjectReference Activate 91804 14 b:false
wait 11
log CHECK door pulled-here once toggled
log CHECK bot logged info\] Activation of 0:91804 relayed here
log CHECK smooth all
wait 3
log CHECK game alive
log DO god off
wait 2
log done
stop
