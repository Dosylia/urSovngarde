# A living NPC holding a torch becomes the bot's copy and its items are set to the bot's: the game lives on.
#
#   headless.ps1 up
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-torch-hand
#
# The game reads the extra data list of a hand that holds a light with no null check (SkyrimVR.exe+0x6896F3, +0x1F0EEB).
# In play, Aela with a torch passed to the other player at a join, her items were set to its items, and the hand guard left her left hand with no list.
# Here Lydia (A2C94) holds a torch (1D4EC); offline, the bot registers her alive; back online she becomes its copy and her torch is taken.
# On the rig the game mostly clears her hand itself; in one run of four it did not, and the hand guard emptied it (`HandWatch: A2C94 ... left hand (1D4EC)`), as in play.
# So a build whose guard leaves such a hand with no list would fail here only now and then: read the `HandWatch:` line before calling a pass a test of the guard.
# A run counts only when (REQUIRE) the bot led and her torch was taken away by the items it set.
party lead
log live torch hand
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 3
stay

log -- night, and Lydia beside the player, waiting, no longer its teammate, a torch she may not put away in her left hand
log DO console set gamehour to 23
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo A2C94 14 f:150 f:0 f:0 b:false
log DO papyrus Actor SetActorValue A2C94 s:WaitingForPlayer f:1
log DO papyrus Actor SetPlayerTeammate A2C94 b:false b:false
log DO papyrus ObjectReference AddItem A2C94 1D4EC n:1 b:true
log DO papyrus Actor EquipItem A2C94 1D4EC b:true b:true
wait 5
# 11 is a torch, in the left hand (0).
log DO papyrus Actor GetEquippedItemType A2C94 n:0
log REQUIRE ref A2C94 count 1D4EC 1

log -- offline (F6) while the bot registers her alive, with no torch
log DO f6
wait 8
npc A2C94 base A2C8E
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 2

log -- back online: she becomes the bot's copy and her items are set to the bot's, which takes the torch out of her hand
log DO f6
wait 15
waitfor known other 60
wait 5
log REQUIRE bot logged info\] Leading the party
log REQUIRE ref A2C94 count 1D4EC 0
# The game's light code runs as she stands near the player: give it time.
wait 15
log CHECK game alive
# Her left hand is empty (0): the game cleared it as the torch left.
log DO papyrus Actor GetEquippedItemType A2C94 n:0
wait 3
log CHECK game alive
log done
stop
