# The control for live-takeover-walk: an NPC this game's AI is already walking must not be stopped at the gain.
# It must pass with and without the stop at the gain: this game's AI walks him on, legs and body together.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-takeover-aiwalk
#
# The same recording and claim, but his AI has an order from before the claim, so it wants him moving as he is gained.
party lead
log live takeover aiwalk
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 3

log -- the game drops its connection (F6), so the party passes to the bot; then it connects again, as a member
log DO f6
wait 10
log DO f6
wait 15
waitfor known other 60
wait 5
log REQUIRE bot logged info\] Leading the party
stay
log DO god on

log -- the real player walks, and the bot records it to replay as its NPC
log DO mark walked player
log DO key w 10000
capture other 12
wait 2
log REQUIRE moved walked atleast 100
log REQUIRE bot logged Captured action names of \w+: \S

log -- the bot's Ralof where the walk began: this game's own Ralof is the copy
npc 2BF9E
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 6
log REQUIRE ref 2BF9E near player 3600

log -- he walks towards the player; his AI gets an order, and the game asks for him mid-walk
log DO sleep 5
log DO papyrus Actor KeepOffsetFromActor npc 14 f:0 f:0 f:0 f:0 f:0 f:0 f:150 f:75
log DO papyrus Actor SetPlayerTeammate npc b:true b:true
log DO sleep 1
log DO mark ran npc
log CHECK npc feet moving 3
log CHECK moved ran atleast 300
replay npc 1
log REQUIRE logged Gained ownership of actor \w+ \(form 2BF9E\)
log CHECK absent Takeover: NPC \w+ .* stopped
log DO papyrus Actor ClearKeepOffsetFromActor npc
wait 5
log CHECK game alive
log DO god off
wait 2
log done
stop
