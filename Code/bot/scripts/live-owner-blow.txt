# Another player's killing blow on an NPC this game owns kills it here, and the death goes back to the other players.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-owner-blow
#
# In play the leader hit a bandit the other game owned 27 times, each a killing blow on its copy, and the owner's game
# never killed it: the copy stood at 1 health, a dead NPC standing.
# Here this game places a Lydia (its own, so it owns her), and the bot, as the other player, hits her for more than her health.
log live owner blow
waitfor connected 45
collect death
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5
stay

log -- a Lydia this game owns, beside the player, neither essential nor protected, as a bandit
log DO papyrus ActorBase SetEssential A2C8E b:false
log DO papyrus ActorBase SetProtected A2C8E b:false
log DO papyrus ActorBase IsEssential A2C8E
log DO papyrus ActorBase IsProtected A2C8E
log DO place A2C8E
wait 8

log -- the bot's blow on her, as another player's: more than her health
hit base:A2C8E -2000
wait 4
log CHECK logged Death sync: player \d+'s blow of 2000 on this game's NPC
log CHECK logged Death sync: health broadcast killed actor
log CHECK logged Death sync: local actor \w+ is now dead, telling the server
log CHECK game alive
wait 2
log done
stop
