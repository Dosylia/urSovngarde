# Walks the player forward, right, back and left, then turns only the headset, so the client logs walk and turn values.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-heading-walk
#
# Each "HeadingMove:" line gives the walk heading, the actor's heading, the graph's Direction and the process direction.
log live heading walk
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

log -- forward
log DO key w 2500
wait 4
log -- right
log DO key d 2500
wait 4
log -- back
log DO key s 2500
wait 4
log -- left
log DO key a 2500
wait 4
log -- standing, the head turns 60 degrees left and back, the hands still
log DO look 60 1000 hold 3000
wait 5
log -- and 60 degrees right
log DO look -60 1000 hold 3000
wait 5
log CHECK logged HeadingMove:
log CHECK game alive
log done
stop
