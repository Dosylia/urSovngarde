# A boss waiting in its ambush furniture, taken over by the party leader's game: does it come out of its idle and move, or stay stuck?
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-boss-ambush-takeover
#
# In play (2026-11-22, Bleak Falls Barrow) the giant spider 3A1E1 reached the host's game as another game's copy in its ceiling hole.
# The server gave her to the party leader 10 s after the leader's arrival, and the leader's AI then had every action refused for minutes.
# Here the bot registers the spider first, in her own cell (371DE) at her placed position, so this game, the party's leader, gets her as a copy and is handed her.
# The bot sends no idle of its own, so whether this game's copy uses her furniture before the hand-over is part of what this shows (`ClaimDiag: ... furniture used here as a copy`).
# It leaves the player inside Bleak Falls Barrow: run it alone, then `headless.ps1 down`.
log live boss ambush takeover
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 3
log REQUIRE bot logged info\] In the party of player \d+ as a member

log -- the bot registers the giant spider of Bleak Falls Barrow in her own cell, where she is placed
teleport 140887 -32896
wait 1
cell 371DE
wait 2
stay
npc 3A1E1
waitfor owned == 1 20
# From the bot's place by the rig's start (140887, -32896, about -10488) to her placed position (3184, -3123, -1796).
npcmove -137703 29773 8692
wait 5

log -- this game goes to her, 400 units from her so that a spider free to move has to come to the player; its player is the party's leader
log DO god on
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo 14 3A1E1 f:0 f:400 f:0 b:false
# Her copy is here about 5 s after the move, and this game is handed her about 18 s after it.
wait 12
log DO mark spider ref 3A1E1
wait 20
log REQUIRE logged TakeoverDiag: NPC 3A1E1 as it is gained
log CHECK logged ClaimDiag: NPC 3A1E1 gained
log CHECK logged Takeover: NPC 3A1E1 sat in its furniture as a copy here by its graph alone; its graph took '\w+' to leave it

log -- once out of her idle (its exit takes a few seconds), her AI's actions must be taken, and she must come to the player
log DO logmark
wait 20
log CHECK absent ActionFlood: actor 3A1E1 performed action
# Stuck, she does not move at all (0 units on a build without the change); free, she drops from her hole and fights, from afar too, as she spits.
log CHECK moved spider atleast 30
log CHECK game alive
wait 3
log done
stop
