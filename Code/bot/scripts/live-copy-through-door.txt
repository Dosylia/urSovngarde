# A persistent NPC its owner takes through a door goes through it in this game too, not left behind where its copy stood.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-copy-through-door
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py --expect-fail live-copy-through-door     on a build without the fix: must be RED
#
# A player went into a fort with Lydia following; in the other player's game Lydia stayed in the lake by the door,
# still swimming, and was seen as a second Lydia outside. The server took her out of that player's view and nothing
# moved that game's own Lydia, the reference her copy was.
# Here the bot takes Ralof (2BF9E, a reference of Skyrim.esm) as its NPC beside the player, so this game's own Ralof
# becomes the copy; then Ralof alone goes into Helgen keep's interior (5DE24), as a follower going through a door.
# The bot leads the party (this game drops its connection and comes back), or this game, as leader, would claim Ralof.
# A run counts only when (REQUIRE) the bot did lead, and this game's Ralof first stood by the player as the copy.
party lead
log live copy through door
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 3

log -- the game drops its connection (F6), so the party passes to the bot; then it connects again, as a member
log DO f6
wait 10
log DO f6
wait 15
waitfor known other 60
wait 5
# "info] " is in the bot's own log line, not in its echo of this check.
log REQUIRE bot logged info\] Leading the party
stay

log -- the bot's Ralof beside the player: this game's own Ralof is the copy
npc 2BF9E
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 8
log REQUIRE ref 2BF9E near player 1500

log -- Ralof goes into Helgen keep, as a follower through a door, the bot staying outside
npcmove 0 0 0 cell 5DE24
wait 8
log CHECK ref 2BF9E cell other
log CHECK logged Successfully teleported actor, form id: 2BF9E
log CHECK game alive
wait 2
log done
stop
