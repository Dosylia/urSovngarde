# A persistent NPC its owner takes indoors stays indoors in this game: no copy left outside, no second one walking home.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-follower-stays-in
#
# Players saw a follower (Lydia) stay outside, or walk home, in the other player's game while her owner took her into a
# building. live-copy-through-door checks the moment she goes in; this checks the half minute after: this game's own
# reference must stay in the other cell, and the desync scan must find no duplicate of her drawn here.
# As there: the bot takes Ralof (2BF9E) as its NPC beside the player, leads the party, and sends him into Helgen keep (5DE24).
party lead
log live follower stays in
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 3

log -- the game drops its connection (F6), so the party passes to the bot; then it connects again, as a member
log DO f6
wait 10
log DO f6
wait 15
waitfor known other 60
wait 5
log REQUIRE bot logged info\] Leading the party
stay

log -- the bot's Ralof beside the player: this game's own Ralof is the copy
npc 2BF9E
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 8
log REQUIRE ref 2BF9E near player 1500

log -- Ralof goes into Helgen keep, as a follower through a door, the bot staying outside
npcmove 0 0 0 cell 5DE24
wait 8
log REQUIRE ref 2BF9E cell other

log -- for half a minute he must stay in there in this game, and nothing of him outside
wait 10
log CHECK ref 2BF9E cell other
wait 10
log CHECK ref 2BF9E cell other
report
wait 2
log CHECK desync all
wait 10
log CHECK ref 2BF9E cell other
log CHECK smooth all
log CHECK game alive
wait 2
log done
stop
