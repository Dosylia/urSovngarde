# A teleport this player cannot see loads nothing.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-teleport-far
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py --expect-fail live-teleport-far     on a build without the fix: must be RED
#
# When a game lets go of an actor it unloaded, the server tells every other player to move that actor to where it was
# last seen (NotifyActorTeleport). A game that moved it there loaded that far exterior cell, and the game's streaming of
# such cells crashed four times on the thread that promotes large references, 3 to 47 ms after bursts of these moves.
# Here: Lydia (A2C94, the follower in the test save) is put next to the player and told to wait; the bot takes her as
# its follower; the player travels far away; the bot lets her go as last seen in a third place, far from both.
# The game must leave her where she is: neither she nor that place is loaded here.
log live teleport far
waitfor connected 45
collect ownership party
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
ownership accept
wait 5

log -- Lydia next to the player, waiting there
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo A2C94 14 f:100 f:0 f:0 b:false
log DO papyrus Actor SetActorValue A2C94 s:WaitingForPlayer f:1
waitfor known ref:A2C94 40
wait 3
claim ref:A2C94 follower
waitfor owned == 1 15
expect owned == 1
stay

log -- the player travels far away; Lydia stays behind, unloaded here
log DO console cow Tamriel 0 0
wait 30

log -- the bot lets her go as last seen far from both
release ref:A2C94 at 90000 40000 0
wait 5
log REQUIRE logged (Successfully teleported actor, form id: A2C94|Teleport of actor A2C94 not made)
log CHECK absent Successfully teleported actor, form id: A2C94,
log CHECK logged Teleport of actor A2C94 not made: it is not loaded here, nor is the place it goes to
log CHECK game alive
log CHECK game inworld
log done
stop
