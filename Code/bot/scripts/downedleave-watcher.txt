# The other half of the nobody-left test: leaves the party while the other side is down.
#
# "party lead": this bot keeps the party the server makes for it, so the other side joins it.
party lead
log downedleave watcher
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn
record downedleave watcher
waitfor known other 40

log -- the other side goes down; this bot leaves the party
waitfor downstate other down 30
expect downstate other down
wait 2
party leave
wait 5
expect connected
record off
report bot-downedleave-watcher.txt
log done
stop
