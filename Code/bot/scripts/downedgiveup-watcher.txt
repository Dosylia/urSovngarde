# The other half of downedgiveup: the party's leader, with a safe point, told the other side respawned; a revive then is refused.
#
# "party lead": this bot keeps the party the server makes for it, so the other side joins it.
party lead
log downedgiveup watcher
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn
record downedgiveup watcher
waitfor known other 40
safepoint 500 600 100
log -- the other side goes down, then its game gives up the wait
waitfor downstate other down 30
expect downstate other down
waitfor downstate other respawned 20
expect downstate other respawned
log -- too late: it is respawning, so a revive is refused
revive other
wait 3
expect refused notdown
expect connected
record off
report bot-downedgiveup-watcher.txt
log done
stop
