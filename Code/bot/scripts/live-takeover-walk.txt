# What a game does with an NPC it takes over while that NPC walks.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-takeover-walk
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py --expect-fail live-takeover-walk     on a build without the fix: must be RED
#
# In play (2026-10-31) NPCs looked wrong on the game that had just gained them.
# Here an NPC gained mid-walk stopped dead and held a stride, its feet rocking 5-28 units from the pelvis, until something moved it.
# The bot's Ralof (2BF9E, a reference of Skyrim.esm) walks towards the player as this game's copy, from a real player's recording.
# Mid-walk the game claims him, through the path of its own follower: setting the teammate flag is what makes it ask.
# That is a party leader claim on the server and the same gained path in the client, except that a follower keeps its gear here.
# `replay` holds the bot's script until it ends, so what happens during the walk is queued for the driver first.
# It is judged on his legs, read from the game: they stand once he stands, and move with him when his AI walks him.
# The takeover and his AI are the real game's, but the copy's source is a bot and its pose a player's, so this proves nothing alone.
# Run Tools\VR\takeover-probe.py beside it for his position, legs and state over time.
party lead
log live takeover walk
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 3

log -- the game drops its connection (F6), so the party passes to the bot; then it connects again, as a member
log DO f6
wait 10
log DO f6
wait 15
waitfor known other 60
wait 5
log REQUIRE bot logged info\] Leading the party
stay
log DO god on

log -- the real player walks, and the bot records it to replay as its NPC
log DO mark walked player
log DO key w 10000
capture other 12
wait 2
log REQUIRE moved walked atleast 100
log REQUIRE bot logged Captured action names of \w+: \S

log -- the bot's Ralof where the walk began: this game's own Ralof is the copy
npc 2BF9E
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 6
log REQUIRE ref 2BF9E near player 3600

log -- he walks towards the player, with the headset on him
log DO look at npc 500 hold 60000
wait 2
log REQUIRE view npc
log DO sleep 4
log SHOT walking of npc
log DO sleep 1
log -- the game asks for him mid-walk
log DO papyrus Actor SetPlayerTeammate npc b:true b:true
log DO sleep 3
log SHOT gained-3s of npc
log DO sleep 4
log SHOT gained-8s of npc
replay npc 1
log REQUIRE logged Gained ownership of actor \w+ \(form 2BF9E\)

log -- he was gained mid-walk and stands: his legs must stand too, not hold the stride
log DO mark stood npc
log CHECK npc feet still 5 15
log CHECK logged Takeover: NPC 2BF9E .* stopped \('moveStop' taken\)
log CHECK moved stood atmost 30
log SHOT gained-20s of npc

log -- his AI walks him: legs and body go together, so he does not slide
log DO papyrus Actor KeepOffsetFromActor npc 14 f:0 f:0 f:0 f:0 f:0 f:0 f:150 f:75
log CHECK npc feet moving 3
log CHECK moved stood atleast 300
log DO papyrus Actor ClearKeepOffsetFromActor npc
wait 20
log CHECK game alive
log DO god off
wait 2
log done
stop
