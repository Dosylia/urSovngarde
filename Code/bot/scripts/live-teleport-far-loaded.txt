# An actor next to the player, let go by its owner far away: no far cell is loaded for it.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-teleport-far-loaded
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py --expect-fail live-teleport-far-loaded     on a build without the fix: must be RED
#
# When a game lets go of an actor, the server tells every other player to move it to where that game had it
# (NotifyActorTeleport). Moving an actor loaded here into an exterior cell beyond the loaded area made the game read
# that cell from disk in the middle of a frame: 622 ms on the MGO list (2026-10-21, a wisp). live-teleport-far covers
# an actor that is not loaded here; this one stands next to the player.
# Here: Lydia (A2C94, the follower in the test save) is put next to the player and told to wait; the bot takes her as
# its follower; the bot lets her go as last seen far from both, while she still stands next to the player.
log live teleport far loaded
waitfor connected 45
collect ownership party
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
ownership accept
wait 5

log -- Lydia next to the player, waiting there
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo A2C94 14 f:100 f:0 f:0 b:false
log DO papyrus Actor SetActorValue A2C94 s:WaitingForPlayer f:1
waitfor known ref:A2C94 40
wait 3
claim ref:A2C94 follower
waitfor owned == 1 15
expect owned == 1
stay
wait 3

log -- the bot lets her go as last seen far from both, while she stands next to the player
release ref:A2C94 at 90000 40000 0
wait 5
log REQUIRE logged (Successfully teleported actor, form id: A2C94|Teleport of actor A2C94 not made)
log CHECK absent Successfully teleported actor, form id: A2C94,
log CHECK logged Teleport of actor A2C94 not made: it goes out beyond the loaded area here
# What the player sees: she is still there, next to the player.
log CHECK ref A2C94 near player 600
log CHECK game alive
wait 3
log done
stop
