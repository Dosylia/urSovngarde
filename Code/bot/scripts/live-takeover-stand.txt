wait 15

wait 5

stay

log -- the real player walks, and the bot records it to replay as its NPC
log DO mark walked player
log DO key w 6000
capture other 8
wait 2
log REQUIRE moved walked atleast 100
log REQUIRE bot logged Captured action names of \w+: \S

log -- the bot's Ralof where the walk began: this game's own Ralof is the copy
npc 2BF9E
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 6

log -- he walks towards the player and stops, with the headset on him
log DO look at npc 500 hold 60000
wait 2
log REQUIRE view npc
replay npc 1

log -- the game asks for him, standing
log DO papyrus Actor SetPlayerTeammate npc b:true b:true
wait 3

wait 5
