# The player's own follower left behind stays theirs, and is theirs at once when she catches up.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-follower
#
# Given to the other player's game when her player's game let go of her, she stood there under an AI with no reason to follow,
# and the two games passed her between them when she caught up.
# Here Lydia (A2C94, the follower in the test save) is told to wait next to the player; the player travels away while this bot stays near her.
# She stays the player's on the server, and the bot shows her as a copy where she stands.
# Without the wait the game takes her along on the trip, as a follower travels with the player, and nothing is left behind.
log live follower
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
ownership accept
stay
wait 5

log DO god on
log -- Lydia next to the player, as the player's follower
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo A2C94 14 f:100 f:0 f:0 b:false
log DO papyrus DialogueFollowerScript SetFollower 750BA A2C94
waitfor known ref:A2C94 40
wait 5

log -- Lydia waits here (the follower's "wait" order)
log DO papyrus Actor SetActorValue A2C94 s:WaitingForPlayer f:1
wait 5

log -- the player leaves; Lydia stays behind, and is not given to this bot
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo 14 35E6E f:0 f:0 f:0 b:false
wait 30
expect ownedby ref:A2C94 other
log CHECK absent Follower A2C94 \(server id \w+\) was given to player

log -- Lydia follows again and catches up with the player
log DO papyrus Actor SetActorValue A2C94 s:WaitingForPlayer f:0
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo A2C94 14 f:100 f:0 f:0 b:false
wait 20
log CHECK fewer 6 Spawn Actor: A2C94
log CHECK absent Follower A2C94 \(server id \w+\) was given to player
log CHECK ref A2C94 near player 1500
log CHECK ref A2C94 enabled
wait 3
log CHECK game alive
log DO god off
wait 3
log done
stop
