# The player's own follower, handed to the other player while this one is away, comes back without a fight.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-follower
#
# When player 1 goes through a door, the server can give player 1's follower (still inside, near player 2) to player 2.
# The follower follows player 1 out anyway, and the two games would pull her back and forth.
# Player 2's game would also dress her in the outfit she has in player 2's own save.
# So the player's game keeps its follower's gear and asks for her back at once.
#
# Here: Lydia (A2C94, the follower in the test save) is put next to the player and told to wait; the player travels away
# while this bot stays and accepts what is left, so the server hands Lydia to the bot; then she is told to follow again
# and brought back next to the player, the way a follower catches up. Without the wait the game takes her along on the
# trip, as a follower travels with the player, and nothing is left behind to hand over (2026-10-12 18:00: every other
# actor went to the bot, Lydia's 3D was rebuilt next to the player at the new place).
log live follower
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
ownership accept
stay
wait 5

log DO god on
log -- Lydia next to the player
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo A2C94 14 f:100 f:0 f:0 b:false
wait 10

log -- Lydia waits here (the follower's "wait" order)
log DO papyrus Actor SetActorValue A2C94 s:WaitingForPlayer f:1
wait 5

log -- the player leaves; Lydia stays with this bot and is given to it
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo 14 35E6E f:0 f:0 f:0 b:false
wait 30
log CHECK logged Actor \w+ is now owned by player
log CHECK logged Follower A2C94 \(server id \w+\) was given to player \w+; not near this player

log -- Lydia follows again and catches up with the player
log DO papyrus Actor SetActorValue A2C94 s:WaitingForPlayer f:0
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo A2C94 14 f:100 f:0 f:0 b:false
wait 20
log CHECK fewer 6 Spawn Actor: A2C94
log CHECK fewer 3 Follower A2C94 .* was given to player
log CHECK logged Gained ownership of actor
wait 3
log CHECK game alive
log DO god off
wait 3
log done
stop
