# Another player's follower is shown on a copy here, and this game's own reference is hidden, not moved.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-follower-copy
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py --expect-fail live-follower-copy     on a build without the copy: must be RED
#
# With her shown on this game's own reference, that reference was moved and rewritten, and stayed so in a save made after it.
# Here the bot's player has Lydia as a follower beside the game's player, and walks her about.
# Then the bot's player dismisses her, and this game's own reference shows her again as any other NPC.
log live follower copy
waitfor connected 45
collect ownership party
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
ownership accept
wait 5

log DO god on
log -- Lydia beside the player, waiting, and no longer its teammate
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo A2C94 14 f:150 f:0 f:0 b:false
log DO papyrus Actor SetActorValue A2C94 s:WaitingForPlayer f:1
log DO papyrus Actor SetPlayerTeammate A2C94 b:false b:false
waitfor known ref:A2C94 40
# Her player's game reports the change after two checks a second apart.
wait 5
log DO mark home ref A2C94

log -- she is the bot's player's follower now
claim ref:A2C94 follower
stay
waitfor ownedby ref:A2C94 me 20
expect ownedby ref:A2C94 me
wait 5
log CHECK logged Follower: A2C94 \(server id \w+\) is player \w+'s; copy \w+ shows her
log CHECK ref A2C94 disabled
# The driver reads the checks as they are logged, so the bot waits before it changes what they read.
wait 2

log -- the bot walks her about, near the player's start outside Mistwatch: this game's own reference does not follow
npcmove 140800 -32668 -10485
wait 4
log CHECK moved home atmost 10
log CHECK ref A2C94 disabled
wait 2

log -- the bot's player dismisses her: the copy goes, and the reference shows her as any other NPC
follower ref:A2C94 off
wait 6
log CHECK logged Ghost: reference A2C94 is a normal local creature again
log CHECK ref A2C94 enabled
log CHECK game alive
log DO god off
wait 2
log done
stop
