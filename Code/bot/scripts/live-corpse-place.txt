# A corpse lies where its owner's corpse lies.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-corpse-place
#
# Each game drops a dead body with its own ragdoll, so the two can land apart.
# The receiving game moves its corpse to where the owner has it when the two are more than 64 units apart.
# InterpolationSystem moves the body, and VRBodySync::PlaceCorpse its ragdoll.
# The bot kills its bear and then reports it 300 units away, the way an owner's corpse that fell elsewhere would be reported.
# The game's bear must be drawn there.
log live corpse place
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn health death ownership
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

log -- a bear of this bot's, killed
npc temp 23A8A
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 8
npclife dead 0
wait 8
log CHECK npc dead
log SHOT before of npc
wait 4

log -- its owner's corpse lies 300 units further north, and the player looks away: a body is not moved in view
log DO look away npc 500 hold 10000
wait 1
log REQUIRE view npc away
npcmove 0 300 0
wait 3
npcmove 0 0 0
wait 6
# What is drawn, the ragdoll, moved there: how far it lay (the gap before), and how far it lies a second after.
log CHECK logged CorpseDiag: \w+'s ragdoll lay \d+ units from where its owner's corpse is; moved there
log CHECK logged CorpseDiag: \w+'s ragdoll now lies ([0-9]|[1-5][0-9]) units from where its owner's corpse is
log SHOT after of npc
wait 4
log CHECK game alive
wait 3
log done
stop
