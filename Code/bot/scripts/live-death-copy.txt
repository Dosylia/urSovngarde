# A copy of another game's NPC dies when its owner says so, falls at once, and lies where the owner's body lies, with nothing jumping.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-death-copy
#
# The bot owns a human (Aspiring Mage, Skyrim.esm 753CB) and kills it (npclife dead), as an owner's game reports a death;
# the game's copy has to follow. A protected NPC is live-death-protected.
# Checked: the copy is dead; it was down within 1.5 s of the death arriving (sampled every few hundred ms); the client logged
# no jank (a corpse moved more than 40 units, a copy dead before its owner's word, a point jump...); the bot's report matches the game.
log live death copy
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

log -- a human of this bot's, alive and on its feet
npc temp 753CB
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 8
log REQUIRE npc alive
log REQUIRE npc standing
# The checks above take the driver a few seconds, while this script goes on: the death waits for them.
wait 6
log DO logmark

log -- the owner's NPC dies; the copy is watched while the death arrives
# The client poses no body out of view, and the bot stands 200 units east of the player, behind the headset on the rig's save.
log DO look at npc 500 hold 16000
wait 1
log REQUIRE view npc
log DO watch npc 10
wait 2
npclife dead 0
wait 12
log REQUIRE npc went down
log CHECK npc dead
log CHECK npc fell within 1500
report
wait 2
log CHECK desync all
log CHECK smooth all
wait 4
log CHECK game alive
log done
stop
