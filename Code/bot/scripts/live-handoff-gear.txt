# An NPC that changes hands keeps the gear its last owner had on it.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-handoff-gear
#
# The bot runs an Anoriath of its own next to the player, dressed as his own outfit dresses him, gives him an iron sword and puts it in his hand.
# The outfit is given as a game reports its NPC's whole inventory: without it the copy here is bare, and this game's AI dresses him once it runs him.
# Then the bot lets him go and this game takes him over.
# In play the game that took an NPC over sent equipment changes for it within a second of most gains, and floods of unequips after one in five.
# This game's copy was left wanting to sheathe: the sheathe forced on an already sheathed copy never played.
# Running him, this game asked to sheathe over a hundred times a second, refused each time.
# Once he is this game's own, the game's GetEquippedWeapon names no weapon although his right hand holds a sword and he draws it, so the hand is not checked after the gain.
# A temporary NPC, so that this game, the party's leader, does not claim him before the bot lets him go.
# A run counts only when (REQUIRE): his copy here held the sword before the hand-off, and this game took him over.
log live handoff gear
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn ownership equipment
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5
stay
ownership refuse
log DO god on
wait 2

log -- the bot runs an Anoriath next to the player, with an iron sword in his hand
npc temp 13B97
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 6
log REQUIRE npc exists
additem 261C0 1 npc
additem 13910 1 npc
additem 13912 1 npc
additem 12EB7 1 npc
wait 3
equip 261C0 npc
equip 13910 npc
equip 13912 npc
equip 12EB7 npc
wait 6
log REQUIRE npc equipped 12EB7
log REQUIRE npc righthand 12EB7
wait 2

log -- the bot lets him go; this game takes him over
log DO logmark
release any
wait 8
log REQUIRE logged Gained ownership of actor \w+ \(form FF[0-9A-F]+\)
log CHECK npc equipped 12EB7
log CHECK fewer 3 Sending equipment request, item: \w+, count: \d+, target object: FF
log CHECK absent ActionFlood: actor FF[0-9A-F]+ performed action \w+ \(Unequip\)
wait 3
log CHECK game alive
log DO god off
wait 2
log done
stop
