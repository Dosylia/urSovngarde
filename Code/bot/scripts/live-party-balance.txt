# Against a party, enemies last longer and hit harder, by the party's players within 50 m of this one (PartyService).
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-party-balance
#
# The game's player and the bot are one party. A Fire Storm (7A82B) from the player hits an NPC this game owns, and one
# from the NPC hits the player; both are measured with the bot's character next to the player and again with it far away.
# With two players near, the player's spell does 1/1.5 of what it does alone (as if the NPC had 150% health), and the
# NPC's does 1.2 times as much.
# Fire Storm bursts around its caster: aimed spells cast by script missed in the rig (the player's goes where the hand
# points). Its damage falls off with distance, so the NPC is restrained and put back before each burst.
# Neither regains health by itself (HealRate 0), or the player's 1% a second of 2000 shows as a smaller loss.
# The player's HealRate stays 0 until the game is closed.
log live party balance
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 3
log DO god off
log DO combat off
log -- an NPC this game owns, at 1000 health, and the player at 2000; neither comes back by itself
log DO place A2C8E
wait 6
log DO papyrus Actor SetActorValue placed s:Health f:1000
log DO papyrus Actor ForceActorValue placed s:HealRate f:0
log DO papyrus Actor SetRestrained placed b:true
log DO papyrus Actor SetActorValue 14 s:Health f:2000
log DO papyrus Actor ForceActorValue 14 s:HealRate f:0
wait 2

log -- the bot next to the player: two of the party within 50 m
infront 400
wait 4
log REQUIRE logged Balance: now 2 of the party within 50 m
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo placed 14 f:0 f:150 f:0 b:false
log DO papyrus Actor RestoreActorValue placed s:Health f:10000
log DO papyrus Actor RestoreActorValue 14 s:Health f:10000
wait 2
log CHECK health placed mark
log DO papyrus Spell Cast 7A82B 14 14
wait 2
log CHECK health placed lost player-near
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo placed 14 f:0 f:150 f:0 b:false
wait 2
log CHECK health 14 mark
log DO papyrus Spell Cast 7A82B placed placed
wait 2
log CHECK health 14 lost npc-near

log -- the bot far away: the player alone
teleport away
wait 6
log REQUIRE logged Balance: now 1 of the party within 50 m
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo placed 14 f:0 f:150 f:0 b:false
log DO papyrus Actor RestoreActorValue placed s:Health f:10000
log DO papyrus Actor RestoreActorValue 14 s:Health f:10000
wait 2
log CHECK health placed mark
log DO papyrus Spell Cast 7A82B 14 14
wait 2
log CHECK health placed lost player-alone
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo placed 14 f:0 f:150 f:0 b:false
wait 2
log CHECK health 14 mark
log DO papyrus Spell Cast 7A82B placed placed
wait 2
log CHECK health 14 lost npc-alone

log CHECK lost player-near over player-alone 0.6 0.73
log CHECK lost npc-near over npc-alone 1.1 1.32
log DO combat on
teleport back
wait 3
log CHECK game alive
log done
stop
