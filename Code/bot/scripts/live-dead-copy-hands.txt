# A dead copy's items set, its revival and an equip from its owner, with its hands checked at each step: nothing breaks, and a right hand is left alone.
#
#   headless.ps1 up -Save <a save with Lydia beside the player> -StartCell ''
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-dead-copy-hands
#
# The play crash this follows (a hand naming extra data that was freed) did not come on the rig, so this is no RED/GREEN test of its guard.
# Enchantment Art Extender reads the right hand's entry in the AI process, and its extra data, on every equip of a living actor.
# Here, as a battle's archer: Lydia (A2C94) holds a hunting bow (13985) and wears steel arrows (1397F).
# Offline, she dies in this game and is switched off and on, and the bot registers her dead, wearing a bow and arrows of its own.
# Back online, her body becomes the bot's copy and its items are set to the bot's; the bot then revives her and she equips the arrows again.
# A run counts only when (REQUIRE) her body was given the bot's items while dead here, and the bot did lead.
party lead
log live dead copy hands
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 3
stay

log -- Lydia beside the player, waiting, no longer its teammate, a bow in her hands and arrows worn
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo A2C94 14 f:150 f:0 f:0 b:false
log DO papyrus Actor SetActorValue A2C94 s:WaitingForPlayer f:1
log DO papyrus Actor SetPlayerTeammate A2C94 b:false b:false
log DO papyrus ObjectReference AddItem A2C94 13985 n:1 b:true
log DO papyrus ObjectReference AddItem A2C94 1397F n:40 b:true
log DO papyrus Actor EquipItem A2C94 13985 b:false b:true
log DO papyrus Actor EquipItem A2C94 1397F b:false b:true
wait 3
log DO papyrus Actor GetEquippedWeapon A2C94 b:false

log -- offline (F6): she dies here and is switched off and on, as a battle's soldiers are between waves
log DO f6
wait 8
log DO papyrus Actor KillEssential A2C94 14
wait 5
log REQUIRE ref A2C94 dead
log DO papyrus ObjectReference Disable A2C94 b:false
wait 3
log DO papyrus ObjectReference Enable A2C94 b:false
wait 5

log -- meanwhile the bot registers her dead, with a bow and 26 arrows of its own, both worn
npc A2C94 base A2C8E dead
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 2
additem 13985 1 npc
additem 1397F 26 npc
equip 13985 npc
equip 1397F noslot npc
wait 2

log -- back online, as a member: her body becomes the bot's copy, and its items are set to the bot's while dead
log DO f6
wait 15
waitfor known other 60
wait 5
log REQUIRE bot logged info\] Leading the party
log REQUIRE ref A2C94 dead
log REQUIRE ref A2C94 count 1397F 26

log -- switched off here; offline while the bot gives her 5 more arrows; back online and switched on: her items are set as her 3D comes, as a soldier's at a new wave
log DO papyrus ObjectReference Disable A2C94 b:false
wait 3
log DO f6
wait 8
additem 1397F 5 npc
wait 2
log DO f6
wait 15
waitfor known other 60
wait 3
log DO papyrus ObjectReference Enable A2C94 b:false
wait 6
log REQUIRE ref A2C94 dead
log REQUIRE ref A2C94 count 1397F 31
# The driver reads the checks as they are logged, so the bot waits before it changes what they read.
wait 3

log -- the bot revives her, and she equips the arrows again
npclife alive 100
wait 4
log REQUIRE logged Death sync: remote actor A2C94 set alive by its owner
equip 1397F noslot npc
wait 5
log REQUIRE logged Equipment sync: remote actor A2C94 equips 0:1397F
log CHECK game alive
# A hand of hers that is right is never let go of.
log CHECK absent HandWatch: A2C94
wait 2
log done
stop
