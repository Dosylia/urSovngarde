# Right after the party leader arrives outdoors, the server waits 10 s before handing the leader the NPCs another game runs.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-npc-grace-outdoors
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py --expect-fail live-npc-grace-outdoors     on a build without the change: must be RED
#
# live-npc-grace arrives indoors.
# Outdoors a game sends its grid shift before its cell entry, and the wait starts at the grid shift.
# The leader's game also claims each NPC it loads, and that claim waits as an assignment does.
# Here: Lydia (A2C94, the follower in the test save) waits at the start cell with the bot, no longer the player's
# teammate. The player travels seventeen cells north, so the bot is given her, and comes back next to her.
log live npc grace outdoors
waitfor connected 45
collect ownership party
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
ownership accept
wait 5

log DO god on
log -- Lydia next to the player, waiting there, and no longer the player's teammate; the bot stays here
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo A2C94 14 f:100 f:0 f:0 b:false
log DO papyrus Actor SetActorValue A2C94 s:WaitingForPlayer f:1
log DO papyrus Actor SetPlayerTeammate A2C94 b:false b:false
waitfor known ref:A2C94 40
stay
wait 3

log -- the player travels seventeen cells north; the bot, still next to her, is given her
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo 14 35E6E f:0 f:0 f:0 b:false
waitfor ownedby ref:A2C94 me 60
expect ownedby ref:A2C94 me
wait 5

log -- the player comes back outdoors: the bot keeps her for a while, then she goes to the leader
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo 14 4AFD9 f:-32 f:-911 f:262 b:false
waitfor nearby other 60
wait 5
expect ownedby ref:A2C94 me
waitfor ownedby ref:A2C94 other 20
expect ownedby ref:A2C94 other
wait 3

log CHECK game alive
log DO papyrus Actor SetActorValue A2C94 s:WaitingForPlayer f:0
log DO papyrus Actor SetPlayerTeammate A2C94 b:true b:false
log DO god off
wait 2
log done
stop
