# A dead body held still in the player's hand keeps being sent, so the other player still sees it in that hand.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-body-hold
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py --expect-fail live-body-hold     on a build without the change: must be RED
#
# The game holding a body sends its bones while it moves, and stopped two seconds after it came to rest.
# A body hanging still from a hand counted as at rest: the other game dropped it to the ground under the hand,
# then pulled it back up into the hand when it moved again.
# Here: the player takes the bot's dead mage with the right hand, drags it, then stands still holding it.
log live body hold
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn health death ownership
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

log -- a human of this bot's (Aspiring Mage, Skyrim.esm 753CB), killed
npc temp 753CB
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 8
npclife dead 0
wait 6
log CHECK npc dead

log -- the player takes it with the right hand and drags it in 15-unit steps, as live-body-grab does
# A step of DO walk takes about 0.4 s in the rig (each SetPosition brings up a loading screen), so 12 steps last about 5 s.
log DO papyrus Actor SheatheWeapon 14
wait 3
log DO grab npc right
wait 4
log DO walk 0 15 0 12
waitfor owned == 0 15
wait 2
expect bonesago mynpc <= 600

log -- the player stands still, holding it: its bones must keep coming
wait 14
expect bonesago mynpc <= 600
log CHECK game alive
wait 3
log done
stop
