# An object placed in the world, moved by hand, is seen moving by the other player -- both ways.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-world-object
#
# Dropped items have their own sync; objects that were always there, such as a bottle or a cart, need this one.
# A client watches the movable objects within 400 units of its player and sends where one goes.
# The other client holds it (keyframed) and follows.
#
# The object is a wooden plate of Skyrim.esm by Mistwatch (reference 92D04, about 1800 units from where the test save
# starts). The player is moved to it; the game moves it the way a hand would (papyrus SetPosition, since the VR
# console refuses "<id>.<command>"), and the bot must hear of that; then the bot moves it, and the game's plate must be
# where the bot put it.
log live world object
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

# The plate is an ordinary reference, in memory only while its cell is loaded: the script before this one in the suite
# (live-whiterun) leaves the player in Whiterun, and from there "moveto 92D04" finds nothing (2026-10-03 13:05).
log -- to Mistwatch, where the plate is
log DO god on
log DO console cow Tamriel 34 -9
wait 30
log -- the player goes to the plate
log DO console player.moveto 92D04
wait 10

log -- the game moves the plate; the bot must hear of it
log DO papyrus ObjectReference SetPosition 92D04 f:139200 f:-33349 f:-9679
wait 1
log DO papyrus ObjectReference SetPosition 92D04 f:139200 f:-33300 f:-9679
waitfor objectmoves >= 1 15
# The client's log reaches the disk a little after the line is written; the check read it too early once (16:11).
wait 2
log CHECK logged WorldObject: 92D04 is being moved here
wait 4

log -- the bot moves the plate: the game holds it and follows
# What the player sees while the bot carries it: the plate stays drawn the whole carry (a move that builds it again blinks).
log CHECK ref 92D04 3d steady 3
moveobject 92D04 139080 -33240 -9600 held
wait 0.5
moveobject 92D04 139060 -33230 -9600 held
wait 0.5
moveobject 92D04 139040 -33220 -9600 held
wait 0.5
moveobject 92D04 139020 -33210 -9600 held
log CHECK ref 92D04 near 139020 -33210 -9600 30
wait 0.5
moveobject 92D04 139020 -33210 -9600 held
wait 0.5
moveobject 92D04 139020 -33210 -9600 rest
log CHECK logged WorldObject: 92D04 is being moved over there; holding it here and following
wait 4
# Where its 3D is drawn when it is let go, against where the bot put it: a plate whose reference moves but whose 3D stays behind
# is not seen moving at all (the players' cup).
log CHECK logged WorldObject: 92D04 was put down over there; handed back to physics here \(0 of its \d+ moves here rebuilt its 3D; its 3D is \d units from the last place it was put\)

# An item one player moves or touches must stay usable for the other player.
# The plate was just held and let go here; the player must still be able to take it.
log -- the player takes the plate the bot let go of
log DO papyrus ObjectReference Activate 92D04 14 b:false
wait 4
log CHECK player has 31941
# A placed object taken that is not a book is carried off by activation sync, not as a world object "taken".
log CHECK absent WorldObject: .* taken here
log CHECK game alive
log DO god off
wait 3
log done
stop
