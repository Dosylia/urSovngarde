# An object placed in the world, moved by hand, is seen moving by the other player -- both ways.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-world-object
#
# Dropped items have their own sync; objects that were always there, such as a bottle or a cart, need this one.
# A client watches the movable objects within 400 units of its player and sends where one goes.
# The other client holds it (keyframed) and follows.
#
# The object is a bottle of Black-Briar Mead of Skyrim.esm at Mistwatch (reference 92CFE, base 2C35A), on FUS and MGO
# alike. (The wooden plate 92D04 used before is gone on MGO: a mod there sinks it to z -30000.) The player is put 150
# units from it, not on it, or the player's body would knock it away. The game moves it the way a hand would (papyrus
# SetPosition, since the VR console refuses "<id>.<command>"), and the bot must hear of that; then the bot moves it,
# and the game's bottle must be where the bot put it.
log live world object
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

# The bottle is an ordinary reference, in memory only while its cell is loaded: the script before this one in the suite
# (live-whiterun) leaves the player in Whiterun, from where Mistwatch's references are not loaded.
log -- to Mistwatch, where the bottle is
log DO god on
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo 14 4AFD9 f:-32 f:-911 f:262 b:false
wait 30
log -- the player goes next to the bottle
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo 14 92CFE f:150 f:0 f:0 b:true
wait 10
log REQUIRE ref 92CFE 3d

log -- the game moves the bottle; the bot must hear of it
log DO papyrus ObjectReference SetPosition 92CFE f:138739 f:-33100 f:-9679
wait 1
log DO papyrus ObjectReference SetPosition 92CFE f:138739 f:-33050 f:-9679
waitfor objectmoves 92CFE >= 1 15
# The client's log reaches the disk a little after the line is written; the check read it too early once.
wait 2
log CHECK logged WorldObject: 92CFE is being moved here
wait 4

log -- the bot moves the bottle: the game holds it and follows
# What the player sees while the bot carries it: the bottle stays drawn the whole carry (a move that builds it again blinks).
log CHECK ref 92CFE 3d steady 3
moveobject 92CFE 138700 -33120 -9600 held
wait 0.5
moveobject 92CFE 138680 -33110 -9600 held
wait 0.5
moveobject 92CFE 138660 -33100 -9600 held
wait 0.5
moveobject 92CFE 138640 -33090 -9600 held
# Seen from above: on MGO the held bottle's reference sits on the ground (65 units down) while its 3D is held where
# the bot put it; the 3D's own place is checked when it is let go, below.
log CHECK ref 92CFE near 138640 -33090 -9600 30 flat
wait 0.5
moveobject 92CFE 138640 -33090 -9600 rest
log CHECK logged WorldObject: 92CFE is being moved over there; holding it here and following
wait 4
# Where its 3D is drawn when it is let go, against where the bot put it: an object whose reference moves but whose 3D
# stays behind is not seen moving at all (the players' cup).
log CHECK logged WorldObject: 92CFE was put down over there; handed back to physics here \(0 of its \d+ moves here rebuilt its 3D; its 3D is \d units from the last place it was put\)

# An item one player moves or touches must stay usable for the other player.
# The bottle was just held and let go here; the player must still be able to take it.
log -- the player takes the bottle the bot let go of
log DO papyrus ObjectReference Activate 92CFE 14 b:false
wait 4
log CHECK player has 2C35A
# A placed object taken that is not a book is carried off by activation sync, not as a world object "taken".
log CHECK absent WorldObject: .* taken here
log CHECK game alive
log DO god off
wait 3
log done
stop
