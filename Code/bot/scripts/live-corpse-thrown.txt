# A dead body moved in its owner's game is reported where its ragdoll lies, not where it died.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-corpse-thrown
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py --expect-fail live-corpse-thrown     on a build without the change: must be RED
#
# A dragged or thrown ragdoll leaves its actor's reference where it died.
# The other games put their copy where the owner says the body is (VRBodySync::PlaceCorpse), once the bones being dragged stop coming.
# So the owner says where the ragdoll lies, and a copy is not put back where the body was picked up.
# Here: the game places an Aspiring Mage (Skyrim.esm 753CB) next to the player and kills it, so the body is the game's own.
# The bot marks where the game reports it, the game throws it with a Havok impulse, and the reported place must follow.
log live corpse thrown
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

log DO god on
log -- a mage of the game's own, killed next to the player
log DO place 753CB
wait 8
log DO papyrus Actor Kill placed
wait 8
waitfor known base:753CB 20
markpos base:753CB
wait 2

log -- the game throws the body; its reference stays where it died
log DO papyrus ObjectReference ApplyHavokImpulse placed f:1 f:0 f:0.6 f:4000
wait 8
expect movedfrommark base:753CB >= 150
log CHECK logged BodyDiag: body \w+ root

wait 3
log CHECK game alive
log DO god off
wait 2
log done
stop
