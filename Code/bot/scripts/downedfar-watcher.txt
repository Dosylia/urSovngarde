# The other half of downedfar: the party's leader stands by until the other side is down, then goes fifteen cells away
# and stays there while the other side goes down a second time.
#
# "party lead": this bot keeps the party the server makes for it, so the other side joins it.
party lead
log downedfar watcher
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn
record downedfar watcher
waitfor known other 40
log -- the other side goes down while this bot stands by
waitfor downstate other down 40
expect downstate other down
wait 2
log -- this bot goes far away during the wait
teleport away
waitfor downstate other died 15
expect downstate other died
log -- staying away while the other side goes down again
wait 15
expect connected
record off
report bot-downedfar-watcher.txt
log done
stop
