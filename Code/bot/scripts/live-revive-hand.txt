# Reviving a downed party member by hand: this bot goes down, and the game's player holds a hand on its character until it is revived.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-revive-hand
#
# ReviveService watches the local hands near a downed party member's body and asks the server after four seconds of holding.
# The rig's controller is moved by DevBench ("DO hold"), so this checks the hand position and the request, not how the gesture feels.
# "DO hold" stands the player with the body straight ahead, so the held hand lands 46 units from it, within the 50 of a touch.
# The hold starts about 2 s after the line and takes four seconds, so a revive within 9 s comes from the held hand.
# Before, the player's heading never turned, the hand missed, and the revive came from the resting hand 15 s later.
log live revive hand
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

# The bot takes the player's height, and scripts before this one can leave the player on a ledge, 700 units over the ground east of it.
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo 14 4AFD9 f:-32 f:-911 f:262 b:false
wait 6
infront 150
wait 3

log -- this bot goes down
down
waitfor downstate me down 10
expect downstate me down
wait 2
log CHECK logged Revive: player \d+ '.*' is down
wait 2

log -- the player holds its right hand on the bot's character for eight seconds
log DO hold copy right 50 8
waitfor downstate me revived 9
expect downstate me revived
wait 3
log CHECK logged Revive: holding a hand on player
log CHECK logged Revive: held a hand on player .* long enough
expect downcount me held >= 1
wait 3
log CHECK game alive
wait 3
log done
stop
