# A corpse whose ragdoll fell through the ground is put back where its owner's corpse lies.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-corpse-fallen
#
# A ragdoll falling through the ground stays over its owner's corpse, so only its depth gives it away.
# The bot kills its bear and then reports it 400 units higher, so the game's ragdoll, once on the bot's spot, lies about that far under its owner's corpse.
# The bot's spot is not exactly where the game's bear lies, so the first move can be across the ground.
# VRBodySync::PlaceCorpse must log CorpseFall and move it up; the body settles back onto the ground each time, so it is left after its moves.
# Moves in a row wait 1, 2, 4 and 8 s, so that takes up to about 20 s.
log live corpse fallen
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn health death ownership
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

log -- a bear of this bot's, killed
npc temp 23A8A
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 8
npclife dead 0
wait 8
log REQUIRE npc dead
log DO look away npc 500 hold 35000
wait 1
log REQUIRE view npc away
log DO logmark

log -- its owner's corpse 400 units higher: the ragdoll here lies that far under it
npcmove 0 0 400
wait 3
npcmove 0 0 0
wait 24
log CHECK logged CorpseFall: \w+'s ragdoll lies \d+ units below where its owner's corpse is
log CHECK logged CorpseDiag: \w+'s ragdoll lay \d+ units from where its owner's corpse is; moved there
log CHECK logged CorpseDiag: \w+'s ragdoll still lies \d+ units from where its owner's corpse is after its moves
log SHOT after of npc
wait 4
log CHECK game alive
wait 3
log done
stop
