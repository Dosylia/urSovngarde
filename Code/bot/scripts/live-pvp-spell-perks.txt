# A player's damage spell on the other player carries the caster's perks, as it does on an NPC.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-pvp-spell-perks
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py --expect-fail live-pvp-spell-perks     on a build without the fix: must be RED
#
# A damage spell runs on the other player's copy in the caster's game, as on an NPC, and each of its hits goes as a health change (PvP on, as on the rig's server).
# The hit carries the caster's perks, which adjust an effect only as the game makes it (AdjustForPerks), and the game's multiplier for hits by the player.
# Here the player gets Augmented Flames (50 % more fire damage in this mod list; F392E, its name is printed) and casts Fireball (1C789,
# 40 fire damage) at this bot's character. With the rig's difficulty (0.6) the bot must receive 36.
# A run counts only when (REQUIRE): the game sent the hit at all.
log live pvp spell perks
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

log -- the player takes Augmented Flames
log DO papyrus Form GetName F392E
log DO console player.addperk F392E
wait 2

# The rig's controllers point straight ahead, and a VR player cannot be turned: the bot's character goes there.
infront 200
wait 3

log -- Fireball at this bot's character
log DO papyrus Spell Cast 1C789 14 copy
wait 6
log REQUIRE logged PvP: hit remote player \w+ for 36
log CHECK bot logged Health change of our [0-9A-F]+ by player \d+: -36\.0
wait 3
log DO console player.removeperk F392E
log CHECK game alive
log done
stop
