# The other half of the party-has-fallen test: goes down once the other side is down, which leaves nobody standing.
# As the party's leader it has a safe point first, and both are told to respawn there.
#
# "party lead": this bot keeps the party the server makes for it, so the other side joins it and the party has two members.
party lead
log partyfall watcher
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn
record partyfall watcher
waitfor known other 40
safepoint 500 600 100

log -- the other side goes down; this bot follows
waitfor downstate other down 30
down
waitfor downstate me fallen 10
expect downstate me fallen
expect downstate other fallen
expect respawnpoint me 500 600
expect respawnpoint other 500 600
expect connected
record off
report bot-partyfall-watcher.txt
log done
stop
