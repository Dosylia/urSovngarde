# A player whose game freezes, crashes or loses its connection mid-step: does their copy walk on the spot?
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-copy-quiet
#
# A copy is drawn from its owner's newest report until the next one arrives. With no next one, the newest report's
# walk played on the spot, every frame, until the server removed the player (about ten seconds after a crash).
# This bot walks a real player's recorded stream, sends nothing for seven seconds, then walks on. The copy's feet are
# read against its pelvis: swinging while it walks, planted while its owner is quiet, swinging again once it walks on.
# The walk on goes out without the recording's actions, so its own moveStart cannot restart the copy's legs.
log live copy quiet
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 6
log CHECK copy exists
wait 2

# God mode: the player is walked forward blind, and Mistwatch has edges to walk off.
log DO god on
wait 3
log -- the real player walks for eight seconds while this bot records six of them, so the recording ends mid-step
log DO key w 8000
capture other 6
log REQUIRE bot logged Captured action names of \w+: .*\bmoveStart\b
wait 3
# Beside the copy, facing the way the recording walks: the copy walks off in front of the headset.
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo 14 copy f:50 f:0 f:0 b:false
wait 3

log -- the recording, as this bot's own character
log DO sleep 2
log REQUIRE copy feet moving 3
replay me 1
log -- nothing sent for seven seconds, right after a step
log DO sleep 3
log CHECK copy feet still 3
quiet 7
# The recording starts from standing, so the first reports back say the copy stands; then it speeds up.
log -- walking on, without the recording's actions
log DO sleep 2
log CHECK copy feet moving 3
replay me 1 noactions
wait 3

log DO god off
wait 3
log done
stop
