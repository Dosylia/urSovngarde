# Another player's held spell goes where its owner aims it, not where the copy's body faces.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-pvp-beam-aim
#
# A held spell is cast again on its caster's copy, and the game aims a copy's spell by its body.
# The real one follows the hand, turned by the caster's spell mods, so it travelled a different way on each screen.
# Its angles now come with the VR pose (VRPose::HasSpellAim).
# Here the bot stands with its back to the player, with no target, as a VR player's hand points where its body does not.
# Without the aim the flame goes away from the player: that half is the control, and is what a build without this does.
# This checks the receiving half only. The bot's aim is computed, so it says nothing of what a real hand sends.
# The bot's aim is level, which a wide flame survives. A thin beam then passes over the player, so Sparks is checked by the angle it takes, and the pictures show it turn.
log live pvp beam aim
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 3
infront 150
wait 3

log -- the control: back to the player, no aim sent, so the flame goes the other way
log DO logmark
cast 12FCD away noaim
wait 4
log SHOT no-aim
cast 12FCD stop
wait 2
log REQUIRE logged Remote spell cast: caster \w+, spell 12FCD
log CHECK absent PvP: player copy \w+ hit this player here

log -- the same with the aim sent: the flame reaches the player
log DO logmark
cast 12FCD away
wait 4
log SHOT aim
cast 12FCD stop
wait 2
log REQUIRE logged Remote spell cast: caster \w+, spell 12FCD
log CHECK logged PvP: player copy \w+ hit this player here

log -- Sparks is a beam, not a flame, and the game aims a beam in its own update: the control
# The flame's burn ticks on for a few seconds, and each tick reads as a touch.
wait 6
log DO logmark
cast 2DD2A away noaim
wait 4
log SHOT sparks-no-aim
cast 2DD2A stop
wait 2
log REQUIRE logged Remote spell cast: caster \w+, spell 2DD2A
log CHECK absent SpellAimDiag: copy \w+
log CHECK absent PvP: player copy \w+ hit this player here

log -- the same with the aim sent: the beam takes it, heading to the player
log DO logmark
cast 2DD2A away
wait 4
log SHOT sparks-aim
cast 2DD2A stop
wait 2
log REQUIRE logged Remote spell cast: caster \w+, spell 2DD2A
log CHECK logged SpellAimDiag: copy \w+'s held spell goes heading -?[\d.]+, pitch 0\.0; this player is -?[01]\.\d degrees off it
log CHECK game alive
log done
stop
