# A downed player is told who is reviving them: the game's player goes down, this bot puts its hand on it, takes it off, then revives it.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-revive-held
#
# The reviver's game says when its hand goes on and comes off (ReviveRequest stages); the server passes that to the downed player only.
# The downed player's game then shows "<name> is reviving you" in the headset, and hides it when the hand comes off or the revive lands.
# The line is an overlay the game's screenshots do not show, so the log lines are what is checked.
log live revive held
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

log -- the player goes down, and hears it
log DO console player.damageav health 100000
waitfor downstate other down 20
expect downstate other down
wait 2
log REQUIRE logged PlayerService: player is down, waiting for a revive
log CHECK logged PlayerService: player is down, waiting for a revive; dropped -?\d+ just before; the down sound playing
wait 2

log -- this bot puts its hand on the player
revive other hold
wait 2
log CHECK logged PlayerService: player \d+ '.+' is reviving this player
log CHECK logged VRBanner: '.+ is reviving you' shown
expect downstate other down

log -- and takes it off
revive other letgo
wait 2
log CHECK logged PlayerService: player \d+ stopped reviving this player

log -- then holds again and revives
revive other hold
wait 2
revive other
waitfor downstate other revived 10
expect downstate other revived
wait 6
log CHECK logged PlayerService: revived by player
log CHECK player alive
wait 3
log CHECK game alive
log done
stop
