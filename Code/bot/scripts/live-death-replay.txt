# How an NPC's death looks on the other player's screen against how it looks on its owner's, side by side in one game.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-death-replay
#
# The rig has one game, so the original and its copy are made to happen in it one after the other:
# 1. This game places an Aspiring Mage (753CB), which it runs, and kills it while sampling its body ("DO record original")
#    and while the bot records what this game sends of it (movement, actions and the moment of death).
# 2. The bot brings a mage of its own and plays that recording on it, death included ("replay npc"). This game shows it
#    as a copy, which is what the other player saw of the original; its body is sampled too ("DO record copy").
# The copy must fall as soon after its death as the original did, lie laid out alike, come to rest as far from where it
# stood, and lie still once down. Both die by Kill() here, so they should match; live-death-replay-blast kills the
# original with a blast, as in a fight, and shows how far the copy then is from it.
log live death replay
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5
log DO god on

log -- the original: a mage this game runs, 150 units ahead
log DO place 753CB
wait 8
log DO papyrus Actor SetRestrained placed b:true
wait 3

log -- it dies while this game samples it and the bot records what this game sends of it
log DO record original placed 12
log DO sleep 2
log DO papyrus Actor Kill placed 14
capture newest 12
log REQUIRE bot logged info\] Captured the death of
log SHOT original of placed

log -- the copy: a mage of the bot's own plays the recording, and this game shows it as a copy
npc captured
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 8
log REQUIRE npc standing
wait 4
log DO record copy npc 14
replay npc
wait 6
log SHOT copy of npc
log CHECK npc dead
log CHECK fall copy within 1500
log CHECK fall copy like original 700
log CHECK pose copy like original 30
log CHECK place copy like original 100
log CHECK still copy 30
log CHECK still original 30
log CHECK smooth all
wait 4
log CHECK game alive
log DO god off
log done
stop
