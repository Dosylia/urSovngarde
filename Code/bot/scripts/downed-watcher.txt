# The reviving half of the revive test, and the one that checks what the party is told.
# It revives the other side once from beside it, then tries from far away, which must leave the other side down and tell this bot why.
#
# "party lead": this bot keeps the party the server makes for it, so the other side joins it; a revive is between party members.
party lead
log downed watcher
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn
record downed watcher
waitfor known other 40

log -- the other side goes down
waitfor downstate other down 30
expect downstate other down

log -- reviving it from beside it
revive other
waitfor downstate other revived 10
expect downstate other revived

log -- down again: this bot walks off before trying
waitfor downstate other down 30
teleport 6000 0
wait 3
revive other
wait 3
expect downstate other down
expect refused toofar

log -- the other side respawns instead
waitfor downstate other respawned 30
expect downstate other respawned

log -- down once more: the other side loads a save, up without a revive
waitfor downstate other down 30
waitfor downstate other up 20
expect downstate other up
expect connected
record off
report bot-downed-watcher.txt
log done
stop
